# GPCP Daily: threats to its inputs, January 2024 – November 2026

**Answer.** Yes. Two inputs have already disrupted production, and two more could stop it from being extended:

- **Already disrupted:**
  - In 2024 the HIRS data used to calibrate GridSat-B1 ended. That paused GPCP V3.3 for more than a year, until NCEI recalibrated GridSat with VIIRS.
  - The IMERG V07-to-V08 switch, now late, is the next thing V3.3 has to wait for.
- **Could stop extension:**
  - The DoD's DMSP SSMIS data. NCEI's 1DD V1.3 uses F17 SSMIS as its only microwave input. Sharing was postponed to September 2026, the Space Force now plans to run DMSP to at least 2028, and NOAA's delivery changed on October 1, 2026.
  - AIRS on the drifting Aqua satellite. A May 2025 plan ended its science in August 2026, but it was still operating on October 30, 2026.

Both versions are still being extended: 1DD V1.3 to August 2026 and V3.3 to December 2025. No producer or archive has announced a substitute or a stop. Overall confidence: **moderate**. The 2024–2026 events come from agency notices, but how each input loss would play out in GPCP's own processing is our inference from the algorithm documents.

## What GPCP Daily is made from

GPCP Daily is derived entirely from other datasets (high confidence). There are two lines, with different inputs:

- **NCEI 1DD V1.3 (the NOAA CDR).** Its input table ([NCEI C-ATBD 01B-35](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/CDRs/Precipitation_GPCP-Daily/AlgorithmDescription_01B-35.pdf), Table 1) lists:
  - RSS SSM/I brightness temperatures (F13, 1996–2008);
  - RSS SSMIS, "data only from F17" (2009 to present);
  - GPCP Monthly V2.3;
  - TOVS (1996–2002), then AIRS V6 (2003 to present);
  - CPC 3-hourly IR.
- **V3.3 Daily (NASA MEaSUREs, at GES DISC and NCEI).** It rescales IMERG to GPCP Monthly V3.3, which itself uses GPCC gauges. IMERG takes its IR from GridSat before February 2000 and from CPC merged IR after. AIRS IR-only estimates fill the high latitudes, and TOVS is used only for 1998 to August 2002 ([V3.3 Daily ATBD](https://docserver.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/public/project/MEaSUREs/GPCP/GPCP_ATBD_V3.3_Daily.pdf); [V3.3 release notes](https://docserver.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/public/project/MEaSUREs/GPCP/Release_Notes.GPCPV3.3.pdf)).

## Production status, pauses and substitutes

- **V3.3 was paused, and has resumed without a notice** (moderate confidence).
  - The pause is documented by the [release notes](https://docserver.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/public/project/MEaSUREs/GPCP/Release_Notes.GPCPV3.3.pdf) ("paused at March 2024 while NCEI is developing a new calibration procedure for the GridSat IR input product") and by the [CMR record](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/collections.json?short_name=GPCPDAY) ("paused at September 2024"). The two sources disagree on the month.
  - Its granules now run to December 2025 ([GES DISC directory](https://measures.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/data/GPCP/GPCPDAY.3.3/)), so the pause text is out of date. No resumption notice was found.
- **NCEI's 1DD V1.3 is current** (moderate confidence).
  - Files run through August 31, 2026 and were created November 9, 2026 ([NCEI listing](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/global-precipitation-climatology-project-gpcp-daily/access/2026/)).
  - NCEI's [ISO record](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ncdc:C01726) labels V1.3 "Version Superseded" by its new V3.3 CDR, but V1.3 files are still being posted.
  - No NCEI notice of a pause or an end was found.
- **Substitutes are planned only in V4.** The [V3.3 ATBD](https://docserver.gesdisc.eosdis.nasa.gov/public/project/MEaSUREs/GPCP/GPCP_ATBD_V3.3_Daily.pdf) plans WSF-M MWI in place of SSMIS, with the transition point not yet set, and AVHRR in place of TOVS/AIRS. [EGU26-5885](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-5885.html) calls V3.3 the last generation before V4, with no date.

## Conflicts left open

- **The month V3.3 paused:** March 2024 in the release notes, September 2024 in the catalog. Neither is current.
- **Aqua/AIRS end of science:** planned for August 26, 2026 in [the May 2025 deck](https://aqua.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/AquaStatus_May2025.pdf). [The AIRS weekly report](https://airsnrt.jpl.nasa.gov/report/latest) shows it still operating on October 30, 2026, and [a Feb 2026 trade report](https://militaryaerospace.com/commercial-aerospace/article/55354691/nasa-seeks-commercial-partners-for-terra-aqua-aura-earth-science-satellite-operations) gives power until September 2027. No current NASA decision page was found.
- **When CPC moved under the Weather Prediction Center:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/27/noaa-merges-climate-prediction-center) reported it done in March 2025, while [Bloomberg Government](https://news.bgov.com/environment-and-energy/us-climate-forecaster-to-be-absorbed-in-weather-agency-reshuffle) said in February 2026 that the merger "is being planned". Neither mentions the merged IR product.
- **Which SSMIS feed 1DD uses now.** NCEI says the [RSS SSM/I–SSMIS CDR's](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/climate-data-records/ssmis-brightness-temperature-rss) "final record extends from 1987–2023". The 2017 C-ATBD names that CDR as 1DD's SSMIS input "to present", yet 1DD files run to August 2026. The feed has probably changed, but no document saying so was found. That feed, and whether it survives FNMOC's October 2026 change, decides how exposed 1DD is.
