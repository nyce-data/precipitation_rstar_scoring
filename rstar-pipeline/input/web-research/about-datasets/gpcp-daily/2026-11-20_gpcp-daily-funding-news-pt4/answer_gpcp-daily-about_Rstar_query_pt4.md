# GPCP Daily: funding, its stability, and news (Part 4)

GPCP Daily has two funding routes. NASA's MEaSUREs program pays for V3.x development: a 2023 award to Ali Behrangi (U. Arizona), with Huffman's Goddard group and SSAI. NOAA pays for routine NCEI production and serving, through the CISESS cooperative agreement with UMD/ESSIC and NCEI's base budget. GPCC's gauge input is paid for by Germany's DWD. **The funding overall is under an emerging threat, not an imminent one** (moderate confidence):
- NASA's FY2026 request would have ended MEaSUREs after CY2026, but Congress did not enact the cut.
- NOAA's FY2027 request would cut NCEI to $52.0M, but the Senate committee's bill gives it $70.0M.
- Both producer groups lost staff in 2025, but no source ties any of it to GPCP.

No news item names GPCP, GPCP Daily or its named people in coverage of cuts. Items name the producing institutions (ESSIC, Goddard, SSAI, CPC, the GEWEX office) and the funding programs only.

## Conflicts

- **How long the MEaSUREs award runs (unresolved).**
  - UA's 2023 "5 more years" and GEWEX's MEaSUREs listing conflict with the FY2026 request's end of current MEaSUREs projects by CY2026.
  - That request was not enacted, and the FY2027 request doesn't name MEaSUREs.
  - No source says whether NASA shortened Behrangi's award.
- **ESSIC staff losses (unresolved).**
  - About 25 ESSIC scientists ("around 10 percent of the center's research faculty") were told their funding would end with the fiscal year ([The Diamondback, 2025-09-23](https://dbknews.com/2025/09/23/umd-earth-science-researcher-funding-cuts); [HELU, 2025-08-18](https://higheredlaborunited.org/2025/08/18/cuts-to-earth-science-at-university-of-maryland-college-park/)).
  - UMD said it was "not aware of any large scale funding cuts to the center".
  - The difference is one of scope: individual soft-money ends against center-wide cuts.
- **ESSIC share (stated).** The Diamondback gives "around 10 percent" of research faculty; HELU gives "more than 10 percent". This answer uses the Diamondback's wording.
- **SSAI's August 2025 layoff count (unresolved, minor).** The official Maryland WARN log gives 79, while the warntracker aggregator lists 228; the run saw warntracker's figure only in a search summary. This answer relies on the official log.
- **House FY2026 Earth Science figure (resolved).** A search summary's $2,153.0M is the enacted figure; the House report gives $1,325,000K.
