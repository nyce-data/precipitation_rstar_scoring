# IMERG Late Run: funding and workforce, Jan 2025 – Nov 2026 (part 4)

**Direct answer.** No public budget line, grant or contract names IMERG or the Late Run. It is paid for inside NASA's Earth Science budget: GPM extended-mission operations (about $20M a year), the PMM science team and NASA-internal work packages for the Goddard IMERG team, and Earth Science Data Systems (ESDS) for GES DISC. PPS's funding route isn't stated anywhere public. Support contracts (SSAI and others) and JAXA's GPM share add to this. Funding has held so far. Congress funded Earth Science at $2,153M for FY2026 and has kept NASA at that level under a CR to Dec 11, 2026. But it is under an **emerging threat** with one **imminent** part:
- **Emerging:** both the FY2026 and FY2027 requests zero GPM's own line after FY2026. GPM's future now rests on a 2026 Senior Review whose results are still unpublished.
- **Imminent:** the planned consolidation of data centers (DAACs), which already touches GES DISC.

No cut, layoff or contract change found is tied by any source to the IMERG team, PPS or GES DISC staff. **Overall confidence: moderate.**

## Stability and threats, by source

- **GPM operations: emerging.** The FY2027 request says the "2023 Senior Review approved extended mission operations for GPM through FY 2026. NASA will allocate GPM funding beyond FY 2026 based on the 2026 Senior Review recommendations" [2] (high).
  - GPM was among the missions reviewed. The review gave every mission "reduced budget targets" and invited proposals to accept "an increased risk of data collection degradation" for lower cost [4][5].
  - Results were due in May 2026 and guidance letters in August 2026 [6]. As of Nov 21, 2026, no results, panel report or GPM target are posted (moderate).
- **Congress.** The FY2026 law gives Science $7.25B and Earth Science $2,153M, with no GPM or ESDS line [3]. For FY2027:
  - the House committee recommends $1,325M for Earth Science;
  - the Senate committee recommends $2,187.9M (and $7.43B for all of Science, against $7.25B for FY2026), rejects "the proposed termination" of missions it names, and says it "awaits the results of the 2026 Senior Review" [9];
  - a CR holds NASA at its FY2026 level of $24.4B through Dec 11, 2026, after last year's "43-day shutdown" [16].

  A House Science minority staff report says NASA adopted the FY2026 request as its spending plan during the 2025 CR [18]. That was budget uncertainty in 2025, though no source ties it to GPM. (High for the figures.)
- **Data systems: imminent consolidation, emerging cut.** The FY2026 request says NASA "will replace the existing Distributed Active Archive Centers (DAACs) with a small number of thematic science-enabling teams" and that MEaSUREs will "complete current projects by the end of CY 2026" [1].
  - ESD's Dec 2025 town hall and its 2026 ESDS slides plan the move of 11 DAACs, GES DISC among them, to thematic teams, with a "study underway" [19][20].
  - In Oct 2026, GES DISC ingest paused for the "Consolidated Ingest and Archive (I&A) system migration" [17]. Neither document names PPS (moderate).
- **PMM science team: emerging, uncertain.** The recompete went ahead (proposals due Nov 20, 2024) [7]. No ROSES-2025 PMM element was listed (moderate).
- **Shutdown, Oct 1 – Nov 12, 2025.** Goddard closed and staff were furloughed [18]. ESDIS kept issuing weekly metrics, noting one product "delayed … due to government shutdown" [21]. No PPS or GES DISC shutdown notice was found. The one PPS NRT outage found (Jan 20, 2026) was a planned storage move [22] (low: absence of evidence).

## Conflicting evidence

- **PMM science team selections (ROSES-2024 A.32), unresolved.** Four NASA sources disagree on the outcome:
  - the Atmosphere and Weather ROSES tables give 4 selected of 121 proposals, $2.5M [34];
  - the Hydrosphere table gives 20 of 121, $12.1M [35] (both table sets dated Aug 2026);
  - the 2024 program slide planned about 43 selections and $25.5M [7];
  - GrantExec's copy of the solicitation says $8.5M for about 43 projects.

  The NSPIRES selection page that would settle it wasn't read. Rely on none of these as the team's size.
