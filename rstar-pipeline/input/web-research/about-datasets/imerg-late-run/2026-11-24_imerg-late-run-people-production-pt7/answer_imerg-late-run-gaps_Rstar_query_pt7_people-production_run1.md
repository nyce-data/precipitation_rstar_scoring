# IMERG Late Run: who produces it, how close they are to retirement, and which contracts pay them (Part 7, Run 1)

**Direct answer.** The Late Run rests on two NASA civil servants and about six non-federal staff.
- **The civil servants:** George Huffman (algorithm lead, GPM Project Scientist, PMM Science Team Lead and Chief of the Code 612 lab) and Erich Stocker (PPS Project Manager and Deputy Project Scientist for Data). Both are probably eligible to retire under FERS now or within 2 years. These are estimates: Huffman is about 71–76 with 14 years of federal service; Stocker has been at GSFC since 1991.
- **The non-federal staff:** the three algorithm contractors (Bolvin, Joyce, Nelkin), estimated at about 59–66, are now Science and Technology Corp (STC) employees. That points to ATMOS (80GSFC26D0002, to 2030) as their paying contract, though no record names them on it. PPS's job-control software comes from George Mason University (Kelley, about 55, and a CEOSR group whose senior members are in their sixties). GMU's likely vehicle, cooperative agreement 80NSSC22M0109, ends 31 August 2027.

No succession plan, PPS headcount, PPS operations lead, PPS contract or PPS funding line is published. **Confidence: moderate** on roles and contracts; **low** on ages and eligibility, which are estimates.

**What it means for the risk codes:**
- **P-2 (retirement-eligible within 2 years):** supported for both civil servants (estimated). Three of the four algorithm contractors are in their sixties, but no federal rule applies to them. Tan and Kelley contradict P-2.
- **P-4 (single point of failure):** supported. Huffman holds four roles at once: IMERG lead, Project Scientist, PMM lead and lab chief. The [Code 612 org chart](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/earth/mesoscale/orgchart/) names no deputy. Stocker has been the only named PPS manager since the TSDIS era, and no PPS deputy or operations lead is named anywhere.

## Team size, succession and departures

- **No published headcount** (P-1, P-4 context):
  - The [PPS project page](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/earth/projects/212/) lists no people.
  - PPS sits in Code 619, the Terrestrial Information Systems Laboratory, which merged with the Global Change Data Center (610.2) in 2022. Its live staff list has 192 records for the whole lab ([Code 619 staff list](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/earth/terrestrialinfo/staff/)).
  - The Code 612 staff list grew from 82 records in February 2025 to 103 in November 2026, as ocean and land staff were added. Only 3 of those staff (Grecu, Huffman, Meneghini) list precipitation as a research interest. The IMERG staff pages now show Org 613, which points to a reorganization ([Code 612 staff, 2025 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208101416/https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/earth/mesoscale/staff)). These are entries on pages, not official counts.
  - The 13-author ATBD is still the only IMERG headcount proxy. Its list is the same in July 2023 and August 2026.
- **No written succession or knowledge-transfer plan** was found for IMERG, PPS, Codes 612/613/619 or the Earth Science Division (supports P-1, by absence). Agency-level context:
  - NASA does have a generic, agency-wide "Sustaining Knowledge Through Transitions" guide (October 2025). It tells leaders to hold "legacy" discussions with departing staff ([NASA APPEL, 2025](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/sustaining-knowledge-through-transitions.pdf)). That partly contradicts P-1 at agency level, but nothing shows it was applied to IMERG or PPS.
  - NASA's OIG cites "the Agency's lack of a workforce planning process" ([NASA OIG, 2025](https://oig.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-report-on-nasas-top-management-and-performance-challenges.pdf)).
  - Goddard's workforce fell "over 40 percent" from March 2020 to January 2026, with about a third of its civilians lost in 2025 alone. A letter enclosed in that report describes "a senior workforce increasingly eligible for retirement" ([NASA OIG IG-26-005, 2026](https://oig.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/final-memorandum-ig-26-005-review-of-goddard-space-flight-centers-transformation-efforts.pdf)). This is P-2 and P-3 context, not IMERG-specific.
- **Departures since 2024:** none among the IMERG or PPS people.
  - The PPS contacts page had the same entries in May 2024 and April 2026.
  - The PMM table's Huffman and Stocker rows are unchanged. Walter Petersen (Ground Validation, MSFC) was dropped between May 2024 and October 2026, with no reason given ([PMM team, May 2024 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20240501224602/https://gpm.nasa.gov/science/pmm-science-team)).
  - Bolvin, still publishing in 2026, contradicts a departure after SAMDA ended.
- **Christopher Kidd: the premise needs correcting.** He is on the V06.3 ATBD of 3 January 2020 and absent from both V07 (12 July 2023) and the August 2026 version, so he left the list between 2020 and 2023 ([ATBD V06.3, 2020](https://gpm.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-05/IMERG_ATBD_V06.3.pdf); [ATBD V07, 2023](https://gpm.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/IMERG_V07_ATBD_final_230712.pdf)).
  - No reason is published.
  - He still publishes IMERG work. A 2025 paper gives GSFC/ESSIC; an August 2026 paper gives the University of Birmingham, but that record isn't linked to his ORCID, so a move is unconfirmed ([OpenAlex, 2024–26 works](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=author.id:A5084463178,from_publication_date:2024-01-01&sort=publication_date:desc)). **Confidence: high** on the dates, none on the reason.

## Contracts and processing

- **SSAI's IMERG staff after SAMDA:**
  - All three now list STC as employer.
  - One source disagrees for Joyce: the metadata of an August 2026 TROPICS-for-IMERG paper still gives him SSAI ([OpenAlex](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=author.id:A5084463178,from_publication_date:2024-01-01&sort=publication_date:desc)). Paper affiliations usually date from submission, so this answer relies on the staff page.
  - ATMOS is STC's GSFC contract. It is a cost-plus-fixed-fee, single-award IDIQ with a $163.1 million maximum ordering value (ceiling); by 2026-11-17 the record gave a total obligation of $19,729,764.22. Its account-level obligation is lower, $14,585,308.22, with $11,064,375.29 outlaid; the record doesn't explain the gap. Its scope is "the full range of atmospheric science research … Earth Science Division-Atmospheres" ([USAspending, 80GSFC26D0002](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/CONT_IDV_80GSFC26D0002_8000/)).
  - A HigherGov snippet gives $4,150,875 obligated directly to the IDV, with no task order ([HigherGov](https://www.highergov.com/idv/80GSFC26D0002); snippet). This is an earlier point in the same running total; the primary record's figure is used here.
  - **The start date conflicts:** NASA's release C25-022 (24 September 2025) gives an effective date of 3 November 2025 ([NASA, 2025](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-awards-atmosphere-research-support-contract/)); USAspending gives a period of 2025-12-01 to 2030-12-01 (signed 2025-10-01). This answer gives both.
  - Neither source names precipitation, IMERG or a task order, and the RFP's scope is described as "clouds, aerosols, and precipitation" ([HigherGov copy of RFP 80GSFC24R0037](https://www.highergov.com/contract-opportunity/atmosphere-support-atmos-request-for-proposal-r-80gsfc24r0037-o-b8c8b/)). So "ATMOS pays them" is an inference (**moderate**). If it holds, the staff have a vehicle to 2030, which contradicts D-1 for this piece.
- **PPS:** no PPS contract, contractor or funding line (GPM project, ESDIS or SMD) is published; three required searches found none.
  - PPS is led by a civil servant.
  - PPS runs its own production system: in March 2024 it restarted radar production "after PPS's production system conversion to RHEL 8" ([PPS contacts, May 2024 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20240521055833/https://storm.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov/storm/PPSContacts.jsp)). That contradicts DIRS-4 for PPS's processing.
  - Its known non-federal staff are GMU's (on site) and KBR's (West). KBR's link to a vehicle isn't documented.
  - Earlier runs' EED-3 and HBG contracts remain the only named PPS-adjacent contracts.
- **GMU CEOSR:**
  - GMU's own pages say its Microwave Remote Sensing group "develops job-control, archiving, and distribution software" for PPS. The work tests new IMERG versions and is "funded by NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center" ([GMU CEOSR](https://ceosr.science.gmu.edu/microwave-remote-sensing-group/); [CEOSR, 2025](https://ceosr.science.gmu.edu/large-rainfall-accumulation-from-hurricane-melissa-2025-as-it-passes-over-jamaica/)).
  - **The likely current vehicle** is NASA–GMU cooperative agreement 80NSSC22M0109: 2022-05-01 to 2027-08-31, $7,683,374 obligated and $5,627,625.82 outlaid (record modified 2026-07-20). Its record describes "a long standing cost effective partnership" on "science data system capabilities" but doesn't name PPS ([USAspending, 80NSSC22M0109](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_80NSSC22M0109_8000/); **moderate**).
  - Its end date in nine months is the nearest contract cliff for PPS software. That is relevant to D-1, but no renewal news was searched.
  - The CEOSR staff who appear in a 2004 NASA–GMU report are aged about 63–65 by degree year (est.). They are university staff, so no federal rule applies (P-2 and P-3 context).
- **PDIR-Now** is computed at PPS: CPC forwards merged geo-IR "to PPS for use in the PDIR computation routines" ([IMERG ATBD, Aug 2026](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/documents/algorithm-information/IMERG-V07-ATBD)). This contradicts DIRS-4 for the IR step, though the IR imagery itself still comes from NOAA CPC.
- **V08:** V08 swaps PDIR-Now for CSU's open GPROF-IR, "designed to provide precipitation estimates … for the upcoming IMERG V08" ([Pfreundschuh et al., arXiv 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07167); preprint). Where it will run isn't stated. The Late Run stays in V07 "hybrid" mode until "no sooner than winter of 2026" ([GPM, Aug 2026](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/news/update-imerg-v08-transition-schedule-aug-2026)).
- **Code on request:** no statement in the ATBD, the technical document, the [GPM Data FAQ](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/faq) or the 2025 V08 slides says the production code is shared on request (supports P-3). Only GPROF-IR is stated as open, which contradicts P-3 for V08's IR part.

## Gaps

- **Stocker's birth year and federal service date:** held only by NASA HR, so his eligibility is an estimate. The same applies to Huffman.
- **The PPS contract and funding line:** NASA GSFC procurement holds them; a records request would settle it.
- **ATMOS task orders and staffing:** STC and GSFC hold them; they are not in USAspending.
- **Whether 80NSSC22M0109 covers PPS, and its renewal:** GMU's or NASA's grant office holds this.
- **Why Kidd left the author list:** unpublished; the IMERG team would know.
- **Unread pages:** `check-by-hand.md` lists the pages that couldn't be read, among them the IPWG-12 V08 slides (sandbox refused) and pages 2–5 of the live staff lists.
