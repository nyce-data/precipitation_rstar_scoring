# ASOS: funding, staff and data access, January 2025 to November 2026

**Direct answer.** ASOS has lost ground but has not been defunded. NOAA still budgets the operation of all 956 units (308 NWS, 570 FAA, 78 DoD). The damage is partial and already happening:
- NWS's procurement line for observations was cut by more than half for FY2026.
- The FAA is moving about 206 of its sites to AWOS-C, and Frankfort, KY has already lost its official climate site.
- The NWS workforce lost about 12 percent of its staff and hired no one from late January to September 2025.
- NCEI's archive has an unannounced hole: its ASOS 1-minute files for August and September 2025 are empty, though the Iowa Environmental Mesonet still serves those months (follow-up). An NCEI data-access alert is also open until 31 December 2026.

Overall rating: **imminent for partial degradation, with no current threat to the network as a whole**. Confidence is high for the documented facts and moderate for what they mean for the observations.

## Funding and program

**Appropriations.** NWS's PAC Observations line holds ASOS sensor and processor refresh; no ASOS-only line exists.
- It fell from $36,200K (FY2025 actual) to $16,200K (FY2026 enacted) ([NOAA, 2026](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)).
- For FY2027, NOAA asks for $23,200K. That is the $16,200K base plus $5,000K for Radar Next and $2,000K for "Observations Recapitalization".
  - A subagent first read the request as $16,200K, which is the base, not the request.
- The House bill gives $43,700K, with report language aimed at radar repair ([House Appropriations, 2026](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119hrpt652/html/CRPT-119hrpt652.htm)). The Senate committee gives $21,200K ([Senate Appropriations, 2026](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/cjs_explanatory_stmt.pdf)). Neither FY2027 report names ASOS.
- The FY2026 Senate report did name it. It "supports NWS's ongoing efforts to prolong the service life of ASOS", and it directed NWS to have "trained human observers" ready during ASOS outages in rural and remote communities ([S. Rept. 119-44](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119srpt44/html/CRPT-119srpt44.htm)).
- NOAA's FY2027 plan also moves ASOS circuits off legacy GSA lines to the Enterprise Infrastructure Solutions contract. No amount is given for this.
- NOAA runs on P.L. 119-103 to 11 December 2026 at the FY2026 rate ([CRS, 2026](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R49353.html)). No source yet says what follows. **High.**

**Network still funded.** The FY2027 justification plans "operations and maintenance of 308 NWS, 570 FAA, and 78 Department of War (DoW) ASOS units" at 96 percent availability, under the tri-agency reimbursable agreement ([NOAA, 2026](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)). No proposal to retire ASOS as a system was found. The "right-sizing" wording in 2026 applies to COOP, not ASOS. **High.**

**FAA.** The FAA's Weather Systems Portfolio funds ASWON sustainment, but neither the FY2026 nor the FY2027 justification mentions ASOS or the AWOS-C conversion ([FAA FY2027 CJ](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/FAA_FY_2027_Budget_Estimates_CJ.pdf), [FAA FY2026 CJ](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2025-05/FAA_FY_2026_Budget_Estimates_CJ.pdf); both read through Wayback copies).
- Portfolio figures: $28,050K for FY2026 (labelled a request in the FY2026 CJ; which column it sits in on the FY2027 CJ is unclear); FY2027 request $35,300K, of which $17,400K is for ASWON Sustainment 2 at 1,247 airports.
- The two documents disagree on FY2025: the FY2027 CJ gives $12,700K and the FY2026 CJ gives $16,700K (full-year CR). Neither explains why. **Moderate.**

**AWOS-C conversion and climate sites.** No public list of the 206–207 sites was found. Neither the NWS equipment spreadsheet (as of 18 Mar 2025) nor FAA's NAS roadmap names them.
- The only climate site documented as affected is **Frankfort, KY (KFFT)**. It has been "offline since late June 2025 due to unresolved power supply issues", was retired as an official climate site, and is to move to AWOS by summer 2027. NWS says AWOS "cannot serve as a direct replacement for official NWS climate archiving" ([NWS Louisville, 2026](https://www.weather.gov/media/lmk/2026/KFFT%20Press%20Release.docx.pdf); [WKYT, 2026](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/07/17/frankfort-loses-its-official-climate-site-nws-retires-equipment-after-prolonged-outage/)).
- **Unresolved conflict:** NWSI 10-1003 (2022) says "Federal sites with an AWOS-C designation or above are suitable for NWS climate products" ([NWS, 2022](https://www.weather.gov/media/directives/010_pdfs/pd01010003curr.pdf)). The Frankfort statement may refer only to the AWOS planned there; the sources don't settle it.

**S. 3923.** The Senate passed it by unanimous consent on 10 November 2026. It was received in the House and "Held at the desk" on 12 November, with no referral or vote. Congress.gov's five related bills are all Senate bills ([Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/3923/all-actions); [S. 3923 ES](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s3923es/html/BILLS-119s3923es.htm)).
- Two 2025 House bills share its name, H.R. 3816 and H.R. 5089 ([H.R. 5089 IH](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr5089ih/html/BILLS-119hr5089ih.htm)). Neither introduced text has ASOS wording.
- *Follow-up:* House Science ordered H.R. 5089 reported (amended) by voice vote on 10 Sep 2025, with no floor action since ([Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5089/all-actions)). Congress.gov doesn't list it as related to S. 3923, and its amended text wasn't read. **High** for the Senate status; moderate on the House side.

**Dashboard.** Section 332 of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 requires a public ASOS/AWOS status and repair dashboard ([P.L. 118-63](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-118publ63/html/PLAW-118publ63.htm)). The FAA published information-collection notices for it on 11 May 2026 and 6 Oct 2026 ([FR 2026-09272](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-05-11/html/2026-09272.htm); [FR 2026-20483](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-20483.pdf)). Whether it is live was not found.

## Staff

**OIG 2025-523 has been published.** The Commerce OIG project announced on 17 July 2025 ([OIG, 2025](https://www.oig.doc.gov/wp-content/OIGPublications/2025-07-17-NWS-Staffing-Announcement-SECURED.pdf)) was issued on 4 November 2026 as OIG-27-004-I ([OIG, 2026](https://www.oig.doc.gov/wp-content/OIGPublications/OIG-27-004-I_FinalReport-SECURED.pdf); [entry page](https://www.oig.doc.gov/about/oversight-areas/national-oceanic-and-atmospheric-administration-noaa/?entry=81656)).
- It found that staffing-related challenges "did not have a measurable impact" on NWS's response to high-impact weather in FY2025.
- 541 employees (about 12 percent) left, 263 of them in forecasting posts, and the vacancy rate "exceeded 16" percent by the end of FY2025.
- NWS "hired no new personnel" from 28 January to 30 September 2025, then hired 78 people from 1 October 2025 to 26 January 2026.
- It does not name ASOS, the AOMC or electronics technicians.
- NWS started FY2025 with 4,380 full-time employees. OPM approved direct hiring of up to 450 in August 2025.
- Its 541 is not the 564 the earlier runs cite (an OIG count of January–April 2025 departures), so the two figures count different things.
- The press gives higher figures: "more than 600" eliminated ([E&E, 2026](https://www.eenews.net/articles/national-weather-service-in-transition-as-hurricane-season-begins/)) and "about 15%" of staff ([CBS, 2026](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/national-weather-service-hurricane-season-less-experienced-staff-missing-data/)). This answer relies on the OIG's count. **High.**

**ASOS-specific posts.**
- The AOMC Section Chief post (GS-14, Office of Systems) was advertised 6–19 November 2026. It oversees "ASOS trouble tickets" ([USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/job/891863400)).
- The NWS Office of Observations director is listed as acting ([NWS](https://www.weather.gov/organization/obs)).
- This partly supports P-4: key single posts are vacant or held on an acting basis, though no source says one person's absence stops the network.
- P-2 isn't tested here: no retirement-eligibility figures for ASOS staff were found. FAA technicians are about 20% retirement-eligible, but that figure is for FAA's whole technician workforce, not ASOS. **Moderate.**

**Rehiring.** NWS advertised direct-hire electronics technician posts in March–April 2026 ([USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/job/862472300)). CBS reported more than 300 onboarded by late June 2026, mostly field staff including technicians ([CBS, 2026](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/national-weather-service-hurricane-season-less-experienced-staff-missing-data/)). E&E reported about 274 hired, still more than 300 below early 2025 ([E&E, 2026](https://www.eenews.net/articles/national-weather-service-in-transition-as-hurricane-season-begins/)). No national technician count was published. Earlier, in May 2025, NWS sought 155 voluntary reassignments, including "16 electronics technicians, who repair Doppler Radars" ([WTTW/CNN, 2025](https://news.wttw.com/2025/05/14/after-staff-cuts-national-weather-service-now-works-fill-155-critical-vacancies-ahead)). **Moderate.**

**Context, not tied to ASOS:**
- NOAA-wide separations far outran hires ([GAO-26-108583](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108583.pdf)).
- Radar repair technicians were among those fired in 2025 ([Washington Post via Spokesman-Review, 2025](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2025/mar/01/national-weather-service-already-had-staff-shortag)).
- An NWS reorganization in 2026 was said to bring "no staff cuts" ([The Hill, 2026](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/5843925-nws-national-weather-service/)).
- No DoD, NCEI-contractor or NWSEO statement tied to ASOS was found.

## Archive and access

**The August–September 2025 1-minute gap.** In NCEI's directories, every page 2 file for August 2025 (page 2 holds the 1-minute precipitation) is listed at 0 bytes, dated 3 September 2025. For example, `asos-1min-pg2-KNYC-202508.dat | 2025-09-03 18:48 | 0` ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/automated-surface-observing-system-one-minute-pg2/access/2025/08/)).
- September 2025 page 2 files are 0 bytes too, and page 1 August files are also empty.
- Neighbouring months hold data: KNYC July 2025 is 4,477,738 bytes and October 2025 is 3,463,902 bytes.
- The downloaded KNYC August file is empty.
- The 5-minute files for August 2025 hold data ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/automated-surface-observing-system-five-minute/access/2025/08/)).
- No notice of this gap was found. **High** that the files are empty.
- *Follow-up (same day):* page 1 for September 2025 is empty too. The Iowa Environmental Mesonet, which processes NCEI's 1-minute archive, serves both months for NYC and JFK, with coverage close to July and October ([follow-up](../2026-11-26_asos-1min-gap-followup/answer.md)). The observations survive outside NCEI, at least at those two stations. Whether NCEI will refill its files is unknown.

**Outages and access.**
- NCEI's OS-upgrade page (August 2025) lists "Automated Surface Observation System (ASOS) Data" among datasets that may have outages. It says ISD, LCDv1 and GSOD "reached end of life" on 29 August ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/operating-system-upgrade-outage)).
- NCEI Data Operations staff later asked users for NOAAPort data from 23–30 August 2025, which NCEI "missed … due to a long-term system outage". They said they were "severely undermanned" ([ldm-users, 2026](https://mailinglists.unidata.ucar.edu/archives/ldm-users/2026/msg00007.html); moderate: a list post, and it doesn't say the data are ASOS reports).
- An NCEI "Data Access Delays" alert runs 16 November–31 December 2026: "many access portals … and data folders are currently unavailable" ([NCEI Alerts](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/alerts)).
- This is during the move to AWS, which runs through spring 2027. NCEI says it retires no datasets ([NCEI FAQ](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/news/cloud-migration-FAQ)).
- During the 2025 shutdown, state climatologists reported losing some NCEI data (press only; ASOS not named).

**After ISD.** ISD has "no update beyond 08/24/2025", and its download services were set to retire on 31 July 2026 ([NESDIS, 2026](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/service-location-change-integrated-surface-data-global-hourly)). GHCNh is current: the Central Park 2026 file on AWS was modified on 26 November 2026, while the AWS ISD buckets hold no 2026 data. The 1-minute and 5-minute 2026 folders run through November. **High.**

**Removal or political pressure.** None was found. The NCEI ASOS and ISD product pages are still captured with status 200 into November 2026, and the 2025 Notice of Changes has no ASOS entry ([NESDIS](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/documents-reports/notice-of-changes/2025-notice-of-changes)). This contradicts D-5, as an absence of evidence. **Moderate.**

**Station outages.** NWS offices posted outages at Augusta ME (KAUG present-weather sensor, "missing rainfall reports"), Springfield IL (KSPI), Lima OH (KAOH) and Benton Harbor MI (KBEH). Most are undated ([NWS Gray](https://www.weather.gov/gyx/AirportWeatherObservationOutages); [NWS Lincoln](https://www.weather.gov/ilx/kspi-outage); [NWS Northern Indiana](https://www.weather.gov/iwx/ASOSOutage)). Houston IAH's rain gauge was damaged by construction around 24 November 2025, putting the official rain record "on hold" ([ABC13, 2025](https://abc13.com/post/national-weather-service-says-theres-no-timeline-fix-damaged-rain-gauge-system-bush-airport/18242038/)). No source ties a named ASOS outage to staffing or funding cuts.

## Gaps

- **The AWOS-C site list** (206–207 sites) and which are climate stations: not published. FAA holds it (Weather Systems Team AJW-141), and NWS's ASOS program office keeps the climate-site list. Ask either, or wait for the S. 3923 assessment if the bill is enacted.
- **FY2027 funding after 11 December 2026:** not yet decided. Congress.gov and CRS will show the next CR or the enacted CJS bill.
- **The Aug–Sep 2025 1-minute data:** the follow-up found that IEM holds both months, checked at NYC and JFK only. Whether NCEI will refill its files is still unknown; ask NCEI (ncei.orders@noaa.gov). The Synoptic lead turned out to be an unrelated December 2025 outage at Synoptic.
- **Workforce counts:** no national ET or AOMC headcount and no retirement-eligibility data for ASOS staff; NWS holds them. DoD ASOS maintainers and NCEI contractor staffing weren't found. The Air Force budget line was seen only on an aggregator, which doesn't tie it to ASOS.
- **Status of the FAA Reauthorization sec. 316 GAO study of ASOS/AWOS:** not found.
- **Pages to check by hand:** the pages that couldn't be read (WaPo 6 Dec 2025, The Hill on staffing, the UAA ISER Alaska availability PDF, NWSEO) are listed in `check-by-hand.md`. The follow-up read the WTTW page.
