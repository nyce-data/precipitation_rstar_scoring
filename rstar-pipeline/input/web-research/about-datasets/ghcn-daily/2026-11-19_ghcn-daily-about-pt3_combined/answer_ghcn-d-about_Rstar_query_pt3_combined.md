# How GHCN-Daily is funded, and what threatens it (2025–2026)

**Direct answer.** GHCN-Daily has no budget line of its own. NOAA pays for it inside the NESDIS line "National Centers for Environmental Information" (NCEI), which was $69.0M enacted for FY2026. NCEI's co-located cooperative institute (CISESS, run by the University of Maryland and NC State) adds partner staff. Funding threats are **emerging**, not enacted. NOAA asked for $52.0M for NCEI in both FY2026 and FY2027, a cut of about a quarter. Congress refused the cut for FY2026, and FY2027 runs on a continuing resolution to December 11, 2026, with the House at $60.0M and the Senate committee at $70.0M. No document plans to end or retire GHCN-Daily: it is still updated daily, and NCEI added new sources in 2026. The threat that has actually happened is to its inputs. When NCEI ended its Global Summary of the Day product on August 29, 2025, GHCN-Daily's international coverage was degraded for months, and it has been only partly restored. Among its sources, Argentina's weather-service layoffs are under way, and COOP, ASOS, USCRN and SNOTEL face proposals or staff losses not yet in effect. Overall confidence: **moderate** (high on the budget figures and NCEI's own logs; lower on how much data was lost, because NOAA has published no figure).

## Funding route

- **No line of its own.** No NOAA budget justification, House or Senate report, FY2026 explanatory statement or CRS report the run read names GHCN, GHCN-Daily or GHCNd; the run searched the text of each for those names. GHCN-Daily sits inside the NESDIS Operations, Research and Facilities activity "National Centers for Environmental Information" ([NOAA, FY2027 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)). Its own cost and staff count are not published. **High** confidence.
- **The NCEI line (context; all of NCEI, not GHCN-Daily):**
  - FY2024 enacted: $70.0M with 202 positions ([NOAA, FY2026 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/NOAA%20FY26%20Congressional%20Justification.pdf)).
  - FY2025 actual: $76.0M in budget authority; obligations were $73.0M ($72,950K), and the CJ gives both.
  - FY2026 enacted: $69.0M.
  - FY2027 request: $52.0M, a decrease of $17.8M from a $69.8M base ([NOAA, FY2027 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)). The cut carries no position change: the CJ shows 185 positions for both FY2026 enacted and the FY2027 request, against 126 positions in FY2025 actual. Positions are budgeted slots, not a headcount.
  - FY2027 House bill: $60.0M ([House, H. Rept. 119-652](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119hrpt652/html/CRPT-119hrpt652.htm)).
  - FY2027 Senate committee: $70.0M, "including not less than $10,000,000 for Regional Climate Services" ([Senate Appropriations, 2026](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/cjs_explanatory_stmt.pdf)).
- **Partner money.** CISESS has two NOAA cooperative agreements, both held by the University of Maryland:
  - NA19NES4320002 ran 2019–2025, with $162.8M obligated ([USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_NA19NES4320002_013/)).
  - NA24NESX432C0001 is a five-year renewal, July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2029. It shows $77.5M obligated, $39.9M spent and 29 subawards worth $26.1M as of its last change on 2026-09-30 ([USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_NA24NESX432C0001_1330/)). NC State announced its share as $95M ([NCICS, 2024](https://ncics.org/cics-news/nc-state-to-receive-95-million-as-part-of-noaa-cooperative-agreement-for-earth-system-studies/)).
  - CISESS-NC's 2022–23 task reports include GHCN-Daily work, but no source says how much of the money goes to it ([CISESS NC, 2023](https://ncics.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CISESS%20NC%20Annual%20Report%202022%E2%80%932023.pdf)). **Moderate.**
  - The administration's April 2025 budget plan included "terminating funding for NOAA's cooperative institutes", and some institutes had "not yet received any of their 2025 funds" ([E&E News, 2025](https://www.eenews.net/articles/trump-cuts-would-cripple-noaas-wide-ranging-science-partnerships/)). The FY2026 law kept "Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes" at $104.1M ([FY2026 explanatory statement](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/fy26_cjs_jes.pdf)). The CISESS renewal's award record was last modified on 2026-09-30, and no source reports it ended.
- **Grants and outside partners:** none found. No record ties NCEI's support contracts (Riverside, regional weather-records solicitations) to GHCN-Daily.

## Threats to GHCN-Daily itself

- **The product continues.** NCEI's own status log lists:
  - v3.32 (March 25, 2025), adding airport daily summaries from the High Plains Regional Climate Center;
  - v3.33 (January 5, 2026), adding the Synoptic Summary of the Day version 2 (SSODv2);
  - v3.34 (January 23, 2026), adding NWS CF6 summaries;
  - v3.35 (October 1, 2026), which "resumes updates for Environment Canada" stations ([NCEI status.txt](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/status.txt)).

  The current version string is "3.35-upd-2026111517", an update started November 15, 2026 ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-version.txt)). Daily update files were posted every day through the October–November 2025 shutdown ([NCEI, Wayback capture 2025-11-11](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111202310id_/https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/superghcnd/)). NCEI is now moving to the cloud: a high-severity alert running November 16–30, 2026 warns of "delayed data archival and slower update cycles for many products", without naming GHCN-Daily ([NCEI Alerts](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/alerts?layers=0)). On NESDIS's 2025 Notice of Changes, the only GHCN-derived item is a duplicate FTP access point for nClimGrid-Daily ([NESDIS, 2025](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/documents-reports/notice-of-changes/2025-notice-of-changes)). **High.**
- **Degraded input, August 2025 onward (already happening).**
  - NCEI began "an extensive operating system upgrade" on August 22. It said GSOD "reached end of life on August 29, and will receive no further updates", along with ISD ([NCEI, Operating System Upgrade Disruptions](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/operating-system-upgrade-outage)). The Met Office dates the ISD stop to August 29, 2025 ([HadISD blog, 2025](https://hadisd.blogspot.com/2025/09)).
  - GSOD is GHCN-Daily source "S", and SSODv2 is described as the "successor to GSOD (source 'S')" ([NCEI readme](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt)).
  - Berkeley Earth wrote that since late August 2025, GHCN-Daily, GHCN-Monthly and GSOD "have been broken or incomplete" ([Berkeley Earth, Sept 2025](https://berkeleyearth.org/september-2025-temperature-update/)), and in April 2026 still "broken or in a degraded state" ([Berkeley Earth, Mar 2026](https://berkeleyearth.org/march-2026-temperature-update/)).
  - Its counts of about 20,000 stations a month normally, about 7,500 in October 2025 and about 6,700 in March 2026 are its own merged inputs, not GHCN-Daily's.
  - The run's own count from NCEI's inventory (a lower bound, low confidence): stations with data reaching 2026 number 8,901 outside the US against 11,458 reaching 2025, a 22% fall, against 8% in the US. From 2024 to 2025 the non-US count fell only about 4% (11,933 to 11,458).
  - NCEI has published no statement of the loss. **Moderate.**
- **Team.** NCEI wrote in March 2025 that "the loss of a significant number of staff resulting from the recent release of probationary employees, the Deferred Resignation Program and employees retiring" ended its monthly climate media calls ([Axios, 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/13/noaa-cancels-global-warming-briefings-cuts)). David Easterling, NCEI's chief of scientific services, left in April 2025. He was "one of 58 employees who have retired or resigned", "about one third of the workforce" ([Asheville Watchdog, 2025](https://avlwatchdog.org/top-climate-scientists-departure-is-sign-of-brain-drain-at-noaa-hub-in-asheville-and-of-deep-cuts-to-research-across-the-country/)). No source ties these losses to GHCN-Daily, and no GAO, Commerce OIG or court record names NCEI or GHCN. **Moderate.**
- **Earlier disruption (2024).** Hurricane Helene disrupted all of NCEI's ingest streams ([FedScoop, 2024](https://fedscoop.com/ncei-working-to-recoup-data-after-helene-outage/)). GHCN-Daily's update files show one 19-day gap, September 27 to October 16, 2024. They show two shorter gaps in early 2025 (January 14–18 and February 3–9) and none from February 10 to November 11, 2025, which takes in August–September 2025 ([NCEI, Wayback capture 2025-11-11](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111202310id_/https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/superghcnd/)). NCEI gives no cause for the 2025 gaps.

## Source networks

- **COOP (emerging).**
  - NWS's request for comments of May 7, 2026 lists "Right-sizing and optimizing the scope and coverage of the existing ~6,700-site COOP network" and leaning on state and private mesonets ([Federal Register 2026-09099](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-09099.pdf); [NWS](https://www.weather.gov/mpx/NWS_COOP_Program_Request_For_Comment)).
  - A sources-sought notice, 1305M2-COOP, closed July 13, 2026. It prices "up to 3,000" sites against the ~6,700-site network. It was read only via [Govly](https://app.govly.com/public/opportunities/16692860), an aggregator. The 3,000 figure suggests a much smaller network but is not a decision.
  - The Federal Register lists no later COOP notice. **High.**
- **ASOS (emerging).** NWS and FAA are moving 206–207 FAA ASOS sites to AWOS-C because FAA funding cannot recondition all 570 FAA ASOS ([NWS ASOS FAQ](https://www.weather.gov/asos/faq.html)). Which of those sites are GHCN-Daily climate sites is not published. **Moderate.**
- **USCRN (emerging).** The FY2026 request would have ended all funding for NOAA's research office. That office runs USCRN, whose "more than 138 stations" include one at Crossville, Tennessee ([Tennessee Lookout, 2025](https://tennesseelookout.com/2025/09/30/map-shows-how-trumps-proposed-noaa-budget-cuts-could-affect-tennessee/)). The FY2026 law kept "Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes" at $104.1M ([FY2026 explanatory statement](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/fy26_cjs_jes.pdf)). The [earlier rain-gauge run](../2026-11-18_noaa-rain-gauge-threats/answer.md) found that the FY2027 request again proposes ending the climate labs. **Moderate.**
- **CoCoRaHS:** no threat found; its NOAA funding route is not public.
- **SNOTEL/SCAN (emerging).**
  - Staff: the NRCS Portland snow office "has shrunken from a staff of 12 to just five" ([OPB, 2025](https://www.opb.org/article/2025/03/11/snow-or-no-northwests-water-outlook-gets-murky-with-federal-cuts/)), and "The NRCS Snow Program is only about 50 people" ([NRCS Idaho, 2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDAFARMERS/bulletins/4003651)).
  - Stations: active SNOTEL rose from 909 (water year 2025) to 919 (2026) ([NRCS](https://wcc.sc.egov.usda.gov/nwcc/yearcount)).
- **RAWS:** no threat found. There are "nearly 2,200" stations ([RAWS Depot](https://raws.nifc.gov/)).
- **Regional Climate Centers:** four of six contracts expired in April 2025 ([NOTUS, 2025](https://www.notus.org/climate-environment/trump-weather-monitoring-funding-lapse-regional-climate-centers)). The High Plains center supplies one GHCN-Daily stream. The FY2027 House and Senate bills each give $6.1M for the RCCs ([H. Rept. 119-652](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119hrpt652/html/CRPT-119hrpt652.htm); [Senate, 2026](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/cjs_explanatory_stmt.pdf)). No saved source says when the 2025 contracts were restored.
- **Environment Canada and ECA&D.**
  - Canada: the daily feed "was unexpectedly discontinued on April 28, 2024" and was restored in v3.35 on October 1, 2026 ([NCEI status.txt](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/status.txt)). No ECCC explanation was found.
  - ECA&D was static from September 20, 2024 to January 20, 2025, and its database is now "updated until: Oct 31, 2026" ([ECA&D](https://www.ecad.eu/)). **High.**
- **WMO exchange (emerging, context).**
  - Money: 59% of WMO's 2026 assessment was outstanding on April 30, 2026 ([WMO FINAC-46](https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/EC-80-inf05-7%285%29-REPORT-OF-THE-CHAIR-OF-THE-FINAC-46_en.pdf)), and its 2026 reorganization lists "68 posts abolished" ([WMO, 2026](https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/WMO%20Secretariat%20Reorganization%20%282026%29.pdf)). That is more than the 26 posts Reuters reported in October 2025, when it also said the US owed over 30 million francs ([Reuters via The Star, 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2025/10/24/un-weather-agency-reviews-priorities-as-funding-falls-short)).
  - US membership: the US withdrawal memo of January 7, 2026 lists the IPCC and UNFCCC but not WMO ([White House, 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/01/withdrawing-the-united-states-from-international-organizations-conventions-and-treaties-that-are-contrary-to-the-interests-of-the-united-states/)), and the US "has so far not withdrawn support" ([Health Policy Watch, 2025](https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wmo-focuses-on-ramping-up-early-warning-system-makes-staff-cuts-amidst-financial-crunch/)).
  - Systems: the GTS "is planned to be decommissioned by 2033" as WIS2 takes over ([WMO](https://community.wmo.int/site/knowledge-hub/programmes-and-initiatives/wmo-information-system-wis/guidance-transition-from-gtswis1-wis2)), and the GCOS secretariat "will cease to operate after 2027" without new funding ([GCOS, 2025](https://public.wmo.int/node/37911)).
  - No source ties these to lost station data.
- **Argentina (imminent).** The government dismissed 140 SMN staff on April 15, 2026 ([La Capital, 2026](https://www.lacapital.com.ar/la-region/despidos-el-servicio-meteorologico-nacional-ya-son-dos-las-estaciones-santa-fe-que-perdieron-personal-n10256935.html)), with up to 240 possible. It had "972 empleados" against an ideal of 1,156. The SMN has 125 stations, fewer than 20 of them automatic ([iProfesional, 2026](https://www.iprofesional.com/actualidad/452572-despediran-140-trabajadores-del-servicio-meteorologico-amenazan-con-paralizar-vuelos)). No station closures are confirmed.

## Gaps

- **NOAA has given no figure for GHCN-Daily's station loss since August 2025.** NCEI holds it; ask ncdc.ghcnd@noaa.gov, or compare monthly station counts in the `by_year` files.
- **GHCN-Daily's own cost and staff count** are not published. NCEI holds them; they could be sought by a records request.
- **FY2027 NCEI funding** depends on the bill that follows the CR, which runs to December 11, 2026.
- **COOP:** no decision after the request for comments. Watch the Federal Register and SAM.gov 1305M2-COOP.
- **Not settled:** which AWOS-C sites are climate sites, the CoCoRaHS funding route, whether the RCC contracts were restored, and why Canada's feed stopped in 2024.
- **The token budget ended the research** (at 13:03), not saturation. A second pass on the saved files afterwards checked every remaining coverage candidate and added 11 facts. It made no new searches, so it couldn't fill gaps that aren't in the saved files.
