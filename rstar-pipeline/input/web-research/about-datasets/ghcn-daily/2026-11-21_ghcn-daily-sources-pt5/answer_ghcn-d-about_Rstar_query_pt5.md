# GHCN-Daily's source networks: cuts, disruptions and end-of-life notices, January 2024 – November 2026

**Direct answer.** The one input loss that is enacted and shows in GHCN-Daily's own files is the end of NCEI's synoptic streams. ISD and GSOD stopped on August 29, 2025. Counted from NCEI's inventory, about 1,371 of the 1,907 WMO-ID stations that were reporting in mid-2025 have no 2026 data, and the replacement stream, SSODv2 (added January 2026), has brought back only part of them. Canada's feed stopped on April 28, 2024 and was restored on October 1, 2026; its cause is still unstated. ECA&D paused for four months (Sep 2024 – Jan 2025) and then resumed. The US threats are smaller and mostly proposed or reversed: the Regional Climate Center contracts lapsed in April 2025, were restored, and were extended to October 2026; ASOS climate sites are being lost to AWOS one at a time; NRCS has cut snow-survey staff. Overall rating: **imminent**, driven by the synoptic stream. Most other sources are emerging threats or have no current threat. Confidence: moderate to high on the events; moderate on the station counts, which this run made itself from NCEI's files.

## The synoptic stream: the loss that shows in GHCN-Daily

NCEI says ISD "reached end of life on August 29, and will receive no further updates", with GHCNh replacing ISD and SSOD replacing GSOD ([NCEI notice](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/operating-system-upgrade-outage)). HadISD's operator confirms the stop on that date ([HadISD, 2025](https://hadisd.blogspot.com/2025/09)). GHCNd v3.33 (January 5, 2026) added SSOD "version 2", the GSOD successor derived from GHCNh, "initially … only to provide updates to stations from 2025 onwards". It promised the full period of record "several weeks later" ([GHCNd status file](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-status.txt)); no later entry says this was done. **High confidence.**

**How many stations were lost (the earlier runs' open question).** NOAA gives no figure. This run compared the inventory saved by the Wayback Machine on August 19, 2025 with the current file (November 13, 2026):
- 38,299 stations had 2025 data; 4,792 of them have no 2026 data. About 2,139 of those are CoCoRaHS IDs, which have normal turnover of 1,785–2,078 a year.
- Network M (WMO-ID stations, the GSOD/SSOD route): 1,371 of 1,907 have no 2026 data.
- Stations in the current file whose last year is 2025: 1,379 in network M, against 34–63 a year in 2021–2024. Outside the US: 2,557, against 400–475 a year. This count starts from the current file rather than the August 2025 one, so it differs slightly from the 1,371 above.
- 590 network-M stations have 2026 data. Only 536 of them were among the 1,907 reporting in mid-2025; the other ~54 are stations that were not.

So roughly 2,000 non-US stations beyond normal turnover stopped in 2025, most of them WMO-ID stations in Russia (539), China (92), Indonesia (85), Argentina (47) and Algeria (45). These are this run's counts from NCEI's files, not an official figure. 2026 is a partial year, so late-arriving data could lower them ([inventory counts](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-inventory.txt)). **Moderate confidence.**

Sampled station files show the mechanism. Russian and Beijing records carried by GSOD (flag S) end after August 24, 2025. Tianjin continues into 2026 under SSODv2 (flag 2), with gaps ([RSM00020046](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/RSM00020046.dly), [CHM00054511](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/CHM00054511.dly), [CHM00054527](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/CHM00054527.dly)). Users saw the same drop: Berkeley Earth reported about 7,500 stations in October 2025 against an expected ~20,000 ([Berkeley Earth, 2025](https://berkeleyearth.org/october-2025-temperature-update/)), and Moyhu had no data for China, Iran or Kazakhstan ([Moyhu, 2025](https://moyhu.blogspot.com/2025/09/update-on-august-global-average-surface.html)). NCEI itself warns that SSOD precipitation "may be underreported" and points users to national met service summaries ([NCEI SSOD page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/global-historical-climatology-network-hourly/synoptic-summary-of-the-day)). So the replacement is partial and of lower quality for precipitation.

WIS2 is not the cause found. WMO plans to retire the GTS by 2033, with gateways re-publishing GTS bulletins in the meantime ([WMO](https://community.wmo.int/site/knowledge-hub/programmes-and-initiatives/wmo-information-system-wis/guidance-transition-from-gtswis1-wis2)). NWS proposed retiring only a GTS file website ([NWS PNS 25-35](https://www.weather.gov/media/notification/pdf_2025/pns25-35_updated_ra4-gifs_aaa.pdf)).

## Non-US sources

**Canada (the earlier runs' open question).** NCEI wrote that "Environment Canada's daily update feed was unexpectedly discontinued on April 28, 2024". On October 1, 2026, v3.35 "resumes updates for Environment Canada stations", re-integrating weekly from Environment Canada's web database and recoding the IDs to network N ([GHCNd status file](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-status.txt)). No cause was found. ECCC's climate-data notices for 2024 list only maintenance windows ([ECCC](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/archived_messages_e.html)). The MSC Datamart list shows an April 9 Internet outage and a data-centre migration with downtime on May 7 and 15, 2024 ([MSC dd_info, May 2024](https://comm.collab.science.gc.ca/mailman3/hyperkitty/list/dd_info@comm.collab.science.gc.ca/2024/5/)); none of these is linked to NCEI. The migration is the nearest event in time, but that link is an inference only. The database GHCNd now re-integrates is itself changing. In March 2026 ECCC moved its URLs to Climate Identifiers ("bookmarks or saved links may no longer work"). In August 2026 it said the historical data of 174 Government of Alberta climate stations "will be permanently removed" ([ECCC](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/archived_messages_e.html)). ECCC's wider budget cuts (about $1.3 billion and 800 positions by 2030) were seen only in a search snippet. **High confidence on the dates; the cause is unknown.**

**ECA&D.** The source "has been static since September 20, 2024" and "resumed monthly updates as of January 20, 2025" ([GHCNd status file](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-status.txt)). ECA&D's news list shows only routine releases over that period ([ECA&D](https://www.ecad.eu/earliernews.php)).

**Australia.** The audit office found the Bureau "partly effective in managing assets in its observing network", with 52–79% of target preventative maintenance done in 2023–24 ([ANAO, 2025](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/performance-audit/bureau-of-meteorologys-management-of-assets-its-observing-network)). Staff are steady: 1,711 ongoing employees at June 2024 and 1,722 at June 2025 ([BoM annual report 2024–25](https://www.bom.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/annual-report-2024-25-organisational-management%5B1%5D.pdf), read via its Wayback copy). The Bureau's troubled website relaunch cost about $96.5 million ([BoM, 2025](https://www.bom.gov.au/work-continues-to-deliver-website-improvements)). Australia's GHCNd data kept flowing: 3,457 AS stations have 2026 data (counted).

**Latin America, Africa and Asia.** WMO told COP31 that records "rest on short-term grants" and that the agreed basic network is "less than 40 per cent in place" ([WMO, 2026](https://wmo.int/node/39224)). WMO cut its staff funding by 7.2%, with US dues for 2024 and 2025 unpaid ([Health Policy Watch, 2025](https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wmo-focuses-on-ramping-up-early-warning-system-makes-staff-cuts-amidst-financial-crunch/)). USAID, a funder of African station projects, was dissolved in July 2025 ([Eos, 2025](https://eos.org/features/building-better-weather-networks)). Argentina's SMN dismissed 140 contracted staff in April 2026 ([iProfesional, 2026](https://www.iprofesional.com/actualidad/452572-despediran-140-trabajadores-del-servicio-meteorologico-amenazan-con-paralizar-vuelos)). These countries reach GHCNd mostly through the synoptic route, so the GSOD loss hits them first. **Moderate confidence; links to particular GHCNd stations are inferred.**

## US sources

**Regional Climate Centers (open question: were the contracts restored?).** Yes. The 2021 contracts' final option year began April 17, 2025 ([NESDIS, 2021](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/noaa-awards-six-ncei-regional-climate-centers-services-contract-awards)). Four centers' funding lapsed that day, and within days Commerce said it "intends to give the regional climate centers another year of funding" ([NOTUS, 2025](https://www.notus.org/climate-environment/trump-administration-restores-funding-regional-climate-center); [SERCC, 2025](https://sercc.com/?p=7432)). Congress fully funded them for FY2026 ([BalancedWx, 2026](https://balancedweather.substack.com/p/bipartisan-budget-plan-rejects-most)). All six contracts now end on 10/16/26 ([USAspending](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_IDV_1332KP21DNEEN0007_1330); [HigherGov](https://www.highergov.com/vehicle/regional-climate-centers-services-idiq-7609/)), with at least one task order running to September 29, 2027. On September 14, 2026 the Southeast RCC announced a "$5 million grant" from NOAA "over the next five years" ([UNC, 2026](https://geography.unc.edu/2026/09/14/southeast-regional-climate-center-awarded-5-million-noaa-grant-to-advance-climate-research-and-services/)). That suggests at least that center is funded past the contract's end, but the announcement doesn't say it replaces the contract. The House FY2027 report recommends $6,100,000 for RCCs ([House CJS report](https://appropriations.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/republicans-appropriations.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/fy27-commerce-justice-science-and-related-agencies-report.pdf)). NCEI now calls the program the Regional Weather Records Center Program ([NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/regional/regional-climate-centers)). This matters to GHCNd directly, because v3.32 (March 2025) added airport Daily Summary Messages "as provided by the High Plains Regional Climate Center". **Moderate confidence:** no new contract after October 2026 was found.

**ASOS to AWOS (open question: which are climate sites?).** No list was found. One case is documented: NWS retired Frankfort, KY as an official climate site. It has been offline since June 2025 and moves to AWOS by summer 2027, which "cannot serve as a direct replacement for official NWS climate archiving" ([NWS Louisville](https://www.weather.gov/lmk/FrankfortASOSRetiringasOfficialNWSClimateSite)). GHCNd added NWS CF6 summaries as a source in v3.34 (January 2026), which adds a second route for airport sites.

**Others.** NOAA assessed downsizing USCRN's soil-moisture sensors only ([NOAA, 2025](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/67359/noaa_67359_DS1.pdf)). Senators reported staff cuts threatening California's manual snow surveys, not SNOTEL stations ([Padilla letter, 2025](https://www.padilla.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/12.22.25-Letter-to-USDA-DOC-on-Diminished-Water_Weather-Forecasting-and-Snowpack-Survey-Capacity.pdf)). Nothing was found for COOP, CoCoRaHS or RAWS in this period beyond the earlier runs.

## What it means for GHCN-Daily

- **Collection:** worldwide coverage outside a few national feeds (US, Australia, Canada, Europe) now depends on SSODv2. 590 WMO-ID stations report in 2026, against 1,907 in mid-2025.
- **Processing:** SSOD daily values are synoptic-derived, so precipitation may be undercounted. Records from 2025 on mix sources S and 2 at the same stations.
- **Reliability:** national feeds have failed and been restored several times (Canada in 2016, 2018 and 2024). Restoration has taken months to 2.5 years.

## Gaps

- Station loss is counted, not published. Only NCEI (ncdc.ghcnd@noaa.gov) can say whether SSODv2's full record was integrated and which stations it will restore.
- Canada's cause: ECCC's MSC data-services unit (ec.dps-client.ec@canada.ca) or NCEI could say.
- The RCC contract after October 16, 2026 (SAM.gov SSN-NCEI-RCC-2025 is a JavaScript page) and the FY2027 outcome after the continuing resolution ends December 11, 2026.
- The ASOS-to-AWOS climate-site list: NWS regional climate focal points; no public list found.
- An NCEI dataset-search snippet lists GSOD coverage to 2026-11-18. This may mean GSOD is now built from SSOD; it wasn't read before the research stop.
- 4 pages are listed in `check-by-hand.md`, plus 1 not tried. The sandbox refused 8 hosts; they are listed in `sites-to-trust.md`, and you can decide whether to allow any of them.
- The research stop came by time (40-minute budget), not because the evidence ran out.
