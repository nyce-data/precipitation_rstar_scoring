# PERSIANN-CDR Daily in the news: cuts, policy and threats (Jan 2024 – Nov 2026)

**No news coverage found names PERSIANN-CDR Daily, its producer CHRS (UC Irvine's Center for Hydrometeorology and Remote Sensing), or its researchers in connection with budget cuts, layoffs, funding reductions, restructuring or threats to the dataset (moderate confidence: about 120 logged searches across news, trade and science press, UC Irvine's pages, court records and NOAA notices found none, but an absence can't be proven and a few paywalled pieces weren't read in full).** The coverage that does exist names only the programs above it (NOAA's research office OAR, its cooperative institutes, its Climate Competitive Research line, NCEI, NASA Earth science), and all of that is about proposals, not cuts aimed at the dataset. The nearest items are a volunteer archive copy of the dataset made in April 2025, and a court record of an unrelated NSF grant held by a CHRS researcher, terminated and then reinstated. **Overall: no current threat to the dataset itself was found; the threats in view are emerging ones to its host archive and its funders' budgets.**

## What the official record shows

- **No retirement notice.** NESDIS's 2025 Notice of Changes retired one Climate Data Record, the ISCCP H-Series cloud CDR ("Product will be decommissioned … This will no longer be updated"). PERSIANN-CDR and the precipitation CDRs are not on that list, nor on the 2026 notices read (S-NPP data ending, legacy GOES-R rainfall rate, HDGM, ISD). The Federal Register has no document matching "PERSIANN" ([NESDIS, ISCCP notice](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/cloud-properties-isccp-h-series-cdr); [NESDIS 2025 Notice of Changes](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/documents-reports/notice-of-changes/2025-notice-of-changes); [NESDIS Notice of Changes tag, 2026](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news-events/search-news?tag=1116); [Federal Register API](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=PERSIANN)). *Moderate: January–June 2026 notices may be incomplete.*
- **Still produced, with a delay.** NCEI's notice still says the product "is experiencing a pause in operations due to processing delays", but daily files through 2026-07-31 were posted on 2026-10-13. The product page still reads "1982–present", with PI Soroosh Sorooshian ([NCEI notice](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/node/8306); [NCEI 2026 files](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/precipitation-persiann/access/2026/); [NCEI product page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/climate-data-records/precipitation-persiann)). NCEI's site-wide cloud move (alert 11/16–11/30/2026) is slowing updates for many products, not only this one. *High.*
- **The host archive's budget (emerging).**
  - NOAA's FY2027 request cuts NCEI by $17.807M, to $52.0M. The cut is measured from a $69.807M FY2027 base; FY2026 enacted was $69.0M. A *Science* search snippet (article unread) gives "$18 million", apparently the same cut rounded; this answer relies on the CJ. It says NOAA will "reduce engagement services, tailored products", but keeps archiving and access as its core mission ([NOAA FY2027 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)).
  - Neither the FY2026 nor the FY2027 justification has a "Climate Data Record" line.
  - The FY2026 request also asked to end OAR's "Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes" (−$102.3M, −204 FTE) and its "Climate Competitive Research" (−$69.6M, −51 FTE). Congress kept OAR in FY2026 ([NOAA FY2026 CJ, OAR-4](https://www.commerce.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/NOAA-FY2026-Congressional-Budget-Submission.pdf); [CRS IF13275](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-07-28_IF13275_2ef80027d87ecf872e321ed150aee97e81f9c127.pdf)).
  - *High for what the requests say; the FY2027 outcome waits on the bill after the CR.*
- **CHRS's own grants.**
  - CHRS's NOAA CPO agreement NA23OAR4310443 ran its full term to 2026-08-31 ($390,000), with no termination recorded ([USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_NA23OAR4310443_1330/)).
  - The UC researchers' class action (*Thakur*) does not name Commerce, NOAA or NASA as defendants.
  - CalMatters reports that "almost all" of more than $900M in terminated UC grants were restored ([CalMatters, 2026-11-16](https://calmatters.org/education/higher-education/2026/11/uc-research/)).
- **One unexplained item.** CHRS's EGU 2026 abstract for a new high-resolution precipitation CDR (PUnet-CDR) "has been withdrawn on 19 May 2026", with no reason given ([EGU26-2633](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-2633.html)).
- **NASA.** The 2026 Senior Review gave every extended Earth mission, GPM included, "reduced budget targets". No outcome has been published ([NASA ESD call](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/earth-science-division/senior-reviews/2026/2026%20ESD%20Senior%20Review%20Call%20for%20Proposals_DRAFT20251219.pdf)).

## What it means for continued production

- **Imminent:** none found. No enacted cut, termination order or retirement notice is aimed at PERSIANN-CDR, CHRS or its CDR funding.
- **Emerging:**
  - the FY2027 request to cut NCEI to $52.0M and to eliminate OAR, its cooperative institutes and its competitive climate research;
  - NASA's reduced Senior Review targets.

  Each would hit the dataset only indirectly, through its host or the grants that fund related CHRS work.
- **Watch:**
  - the processing pause, which has been up since September 2025 while files still arrive late;
  - the end of NA23OAR4310443 with no successor found.

  Neither has been reported in the press.
