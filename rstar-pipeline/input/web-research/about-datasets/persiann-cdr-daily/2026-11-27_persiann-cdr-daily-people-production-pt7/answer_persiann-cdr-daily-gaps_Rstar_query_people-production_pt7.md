# PERSIANN-CDR Daily, Part 7: people, retirement, and who runs and pays for the quarterly extension

**Short answer.** PERSIANN-CDR's founder and its named data producer have both retired.
- Founder and PI of record: Soroosh Sorooshian is now "Distinguished Professor Emeritus" ([UCI Directory](https://directory.uci.edu/query/sorooshian?filter=all)). He handed the CHRS directorship to Amir AghaKouchak between April and July 2025.
- Named producer: the only person named in the newest PERSIANN-CDR file metadata, Dan Braithwaite (creator email `dbraithw at uci.edu`, files dated 2026-10-13), is listed by UC Irvine as a "UCI Retiree" ([UCI Directory](https://directory.uci.edu/query/braithwaite?filter=all); [NCEI ERDDAP](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/erddap/info/cdr_persiann_by_time_lon_lat/index.html)).
- Still active: Kuo-lin Hsu (Professor and CHRS Associate Director; estimated age 56–61, so probably already eligible to retire under UC's published age-55 rule). AghaKouchak and Phu Nguyen are probably in their 40s.

No public record names who funds the quarterly extension or which computers run it. No source read here names a successor to NA23OAR4310443, no CISESS or Riverside money for it, and no succession plan. **Confidence: moderate.** The staffing facts come from UCI's own directory. The funding findings are mostly absences.

**UC retirement rule (q1b).** UC publishes one, and it is not a mandatory age.
- 1976 Tier members may retire "when you reach age 50 and leave UC employment with at least five years of service credit" ([UCnet](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/benefits/retirement/ucrp-1976-tier/)).
- 2013 and 2016 Tier members may retire at 55, also with five years' service ([UCnet, 2013 Tier](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/benefits/retirement/ucrp-2013-tier/); [UC Complete Guide](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/wp-content/uploads/forms/pdf/complete-retirement-benefits-guide-for-employees.pdf)). A handbook snippet the Checker saw but didn't read says the Modified 2013 Tier also allows 50.
- UC rescinded its mandatory retirement age (Standing Order 103.6) ([UCOP 2022](https://www.ucop.edu/academic-personnel-programs/_files/apm/apm-issuance-regents-standing-order-tech-rev/2022-04-20-apm-issue-cvr-ltr-regents-standing-order-tech-rev.pdf)).
- Which tier each person is in is not published. Federal rules don't apply.

- **Director change.** CHRS's own directory names Sorooshian "Director of CHRS" on 2025-04-17 and AghaKouchak on 2025-07-13, with Sorooshian then "Founding Director" ([Wayback, Apr 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250417234601/https://chrs.web.uci.edu/chrs_directory.php); [Jul 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250713224301/https://chrs.web.uci.edu/chrs_directory.php)). Supports P-2 for the founder; the handover itself weakly contradicts P-1.
- **Who runs the extension.**
  - The 2026 ERDDAP attributes name one creator, Braithwaite, now a UCI retiree.
  - NCEI's ISO record (July 2026) still names Sorooshian, "Director and Professor", as PI ([NCEI ISO metadata](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/metadata/geoportal/rest/metadata/item/gov.noaa.ncdc:C00854/xml)). Neither record was updated after the 2025 changes.
  - CHRS lists two staff in May 2026, Diane Hohnbaum and Tu Thanh Ung. Ung is the one "Program & System Administrator" (an Assistant Specialist) and is credited with "Methodology, Software" on the 2026 CCS-CDR V2.0 paper.
  - So the only published producer is a single, retired person, and the metadata may be a fixed template. Supports P-4 and P-2 (moderate).
- **How old CHRS is.** Sorooshian's 2020 CV lists "2003-present Director, Center for Hydrometeorology & Remote Sensing (CHRS)", and his group moved from Arizona in July 2003 ([CV via Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20220104041701/https://engineering.uci.edu/files/ssorooshian_hssoe_cv_august_2020.pdf); [UCI profile](https://faculty.uci.edu/profile/?facultyId=5082)). That makes CHRS about 23 years old.
- **Succession or knowledge transfer.** No plan is published on CHRS, UCI, Samueli or NCEI pages, the C-ATBD or the 2025 User Guide. Supports P-1 and P-3 (moderate: an absence).

## Production (q4)

- **Code.** NCEI holds one code package, `SourceCode_01B-16.tar.gz` (posted 2017-03-10). Its README says "To do the bias corrections matlab will be required".
  - The C-ATBD describes "LINUX servers … CENTOS 5.5" with "C, C++, bash shell, and MATLAB" on "3 different systems".
  - The 2025 User Guide still describes MATLAB ([NCEI PERSIANN-CDR documents](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/CDRs/PERSIANN/)).
  - No newer code, operational description, official repository or ANN training code is published. Whether current code differs is unknown. Supports P-3 (moderate).
- **Computers.** No page names them. The job is small: the 2014 C-ATBD gives about 125 GB a year of compressed raw input, reduced to 5 GB of model inputs, and 5.5 GB a year of output.
  - HPC3 (253 nodes, January 2026) gives each UCI faculty member about 200,000 free core-hours a year.
  - Beyond that, it charges recharge for storage ($100/TB/5 years) and grant-bought "condo" nodes or $0.01 per core-hour ([RCIC](https://rcic.uci.edu/_static/RCIC-description.pdf)).
  - No RCIC page names CHRS. Nothing shows CHRS needs outside processing: DIRS-4 is not supported, but this is unpublished either way.
- **MATLAB.** UCI's OIT gives MATLAB to faculty "no cost to students and faculty" ([UCI Engineering](https://laptops.eng.uci.edu/engineering-software/matlab)), and "RCIC contributes to the UCI-wide license" ([RCIC](https://rcic.uci.edu/software/software.html)). The commercial dependence is covered by a campus licence for now. It would fall away if production left UCI.
