# USCRN Daily (daily01): people, team size and funding (Part 3)

**Direct answer.** USCRN Daily depends on a small group of people.
- **Leads:**
  - Howard Diamond (ARL): still named USCRN Program Manager, now also an ARL division director.
  - Michael Palecki (NCEI): the federal science lead for processing and QC.
  - Ronald Leeper (CISESS, NC State): leads the QA and soil-moisture tasks.
  - John Kochendorfer: ATDD's head, now deputy of the "SORD-ATD" office.
- **Field team:** eight technicians, led by Brent French. They are not federal staff: they were ORAU staff, and since late 2025 they are listed as cooperative-institute staff.

Two of the federal leads, Diamond and Palecki, and ATDD scientist Tilden Meyers are likely already eligible to retire under FERS (estimates). No succession plan was found. No document read gives a dollar amount for USCRN's own base funding: it is paid from OAR's climate data line (ARL), NCEI's line and whole-institute cooperative agreements. Even before 2025, three budget requests in a row (FY2019–FY2021) proposed closing ARL, including its Oak Ridge campus, and moving USCRN to "other NOAA laboratories"; none took effect. Confidence: moderate overall. It is high for who holds the roles and low for the funding amounts.

## Team size, history and who could step in

- **Field team:** ATDD's "small team of eight technicians and engineers" does every station's annual visit, and "the majority" have been with ARL "15 years or more" ([ARL, USCRN at ARL](https://www.arl.noaa.gov/news-pubs/arl-news-stories/uscrn-at-arl/), undated, about 2023). It names Brent French as lead, plus Tony Hamby and Josh Blackwell. **Moderate.**
- **Who employs them:** all three are listed as **ORAU** staff in Dec 2024 and as **"Cooperative Institute"** staff from Oct 2025. The change came between the June and Oct 2025 captures; the institute is not named. [ORAU](https://orau.org/blog/programs/video-behind-the-scenes-maintaining-the-u.s.-climate-reference-network.html) describes the stations as managed by "ORAU's team" at ATDD. The field work therefore rests on a non-federal staffing vehicle whose award number wasn't found. **Moderate.**
- **ATDD office roster** (entries listed on the page, not an official headcount, and not all on USCRN):
  - Dec 2024: 28 entries (8 federal, 16 ORAU, 4 contractor).
  - Nov 2026: 21 entries (7 federal, 12 cooperative institute, 2 contractor).
  - ATDD was still a division under Kochendorfer on 13 Sept 2026. The live page shows it as an office of the Special Research and Operations Division (director Walter Schalk). This change is after January 2025, so it is noted for the later run.
- **History:** Diamond and Palecki have held the PM and science-lead roles since at least FY10. Hall, Meyers, Baker and Embler were on both the FY10 and FY19 lists. FY19 thanks 17 team members besides the four leads.
- **Who could step in:**
  - Field: the eight-person team has depth (several with 15+ years). Eleven other non-federal and seven federal staff are in the Oak Ridge office. No deputy to French is named.
  - Science and QC: the FY19 Climate Science Team is Palecki, Leeper and Rennie, so Leeper and Rennie are the obvious backups for Palecki.
  - Program manager: no deputy is named for Diamond. The NCEI contacts page names him alone.
- **Plans:** no USCRN succession, workforce or knowledge-transfer plan was found, and no OIG or GAO audit of USCRN. ARL's lab-review documents flag single-PI programs. According to a search summary of those PDFs (not read: HTTP 403), ARL answered that civil-service rules bar naming successors in advance. **Low** (absence of evidence).

**Proposed ARL closures before 2025 (high):** NOAA's own requests proposed closing ARL three years in a row. Each said that USCRN and ARL's other observing networks under OAR Climate Research "will be consolidated into other NOAA laboratories". ARL and ATDD were still running USCRN in Dec 2024, so none took effect. The amounts are ARL-wide, not USCRN's.
- FY2019: −$4,377K, −34 FTE ([FY2019 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/legacy/document/2019/Nov/FY19-NOAA-CJ.pdf)).
- FY2020: −$4,843K, −35 FTE / −34 positions. This request also names closing the College Park headquarters and the Oak Ridge, Idaho Falls, Las Vegas and Mercury campuses ([FY2020 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/legacy/document/2019/Nov/NOAA-FY20-Congressional-Justification.pdf)).
- FY2021: −$4,979K, −34 FTE / −35 positions ([FY2021 CJ, p. OAR-52](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/legacy/document/2020/Mar/508%20Compliant_FY21%20NOAA%20CJ.pdf)).

Earlier funding shocks (**low**):
- The FY08 annual report mentions an FY2006 "budget hiatus" that delayed deployment ([FY08 report](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/uscrn/publications/annual_reports/FY08_USCRN_Annual_Report.pdf)).
- In a 2005 House hearing, a member said $10 million of the request was removed "for climate reference" ([House hearing 2005](https://www.congress.gov/109/chrg/CHRG-109hhrg99903/CHRG-109hhrg99903.pdf)).
- The FY2006 request added $7.4M to "ensure continuation" of networks such as CRN ([S. Hrg. 109-1094](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-109shrg62359/pdf/CHRG-109shrg62359.pdf)).
