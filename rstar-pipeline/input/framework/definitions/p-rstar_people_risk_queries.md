# People Risk

## People Risk defined

Vulnerability created when critical institutional knowledge resides in a small number of individuals. When a lead data curator retires without a documented succession plan, or a wave of RIFs eliminates the team that knows how to run a specialized processing chain, the data may still technically exist while becoming operationally inaccessible. That kind of loss is invisible to systems that only track files and metadata. P makes it visible.

P covers the people who produce this dataset: its own team, including contractors, at the producer. Staff losses at the wider agency, or at a source network, are context here unless a source names this team. Losses at a source network can still count in DIRS-2.

## Subcategories

### P-1

#### Description

No documented succession plan

No written plan identifies a successor or outlines knowledge transfer steps for critical roles

#### How to find

Search for the number of people in a dataset group and how long it has existed to get an estimate of how many people are available to be a successor. Look for a succession plan, but also look for documentation of knowledge needed to keep taking measurements, process the data, and run the group.

#### What counts as evidence

A written succession plan, or documentation that would let someone new take over: how the measurements are taken, how the data are processed and how the group is run. The size of the team and how long it has existed also show whether someone could step in.

### P-2

#### Description

Key personnel retirement-eligible

One or more people critical to production or QC eligible to retire within 2 years

#### How to find

Search for people in charge of the dataset group and data processing. Search for how QC is done and by who. Look for staffing numbers and mentions of retirements. If find people, find when they started working with the dataset and if they've published papers (get ORCID), what is the earliest year, to get an estimate of their age.

#### What counts as evidence

Who leads the dataset and who processes and quality-controls it, how many staff there are, and any announced retirements. When ages aren't published, the year a person started on the dataset or published their first paper gives a rough idea of how close they are to retiring.

### P-3

#### Description

Critical undocumented knowledge

Processing, calibration, or QC expertise held by individual(s) but not captured in documentation or code

#### How to find

Want to know if a person stops working with the dataset, will another person be able to continue making the dataset. Search to find publically available software, dataset processing documentation, or code to process the dataset and peform QC. Want information on whether there is no documentation on how to process the dataset or there is.

#### What counts as evidence

Whether the processing and quality-control steps are written down, in public code, software, processing documents or methods papers. If they are, someone new could keep the dataset going after a person leaves. If not, the knowledge sits with individuals.

### P-4

#### Description

Single person is point of failure

Loss of one individual would halt production, QC, or distribution

#### How to find

Search for people in charge of the dataset group and data processing. Search for how quality control (QC) is done and by who. Look for staffing numbers and mentions of retirements or layoffs that would impact the dataset from being measured and/or processed.

#### What counts as evidence

Who runs the dataset's production, quality control and distribution, and how many people share each job. Also staffing numbers, retirements or layoffs that would stop the data being measured or processed if one person left.
