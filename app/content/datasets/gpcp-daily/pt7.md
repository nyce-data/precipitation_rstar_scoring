GPCP Daily now rests on about six active people: Huffman at NASA Goddard, Bolvin and Nelkin, who are now contractors with Science and Technology Corp (STC) rather than SSAI, Adler and Gu at UMD/ESSIC, and Behrangi at the University of Arizona as PI. Wang and Sapiano have left the work. These are estimates: Huffman is the only federal employee, he is likely about 71–76, and he has very likely been eligible to retire under FERS for years. Adler is about 78–83 and Bolvin and Nelkin about 60–65.

Both daily versions are still being produced:
- **1DD V1.3:** NCEI received files through 31 August 2026 on 9 November 2026.
- **V3.3:** after the September 2024 pause, NASA's records show the files were extended through 2025 between February and May 2026.

No source names the computers either version runs on. No source publishes a succession plan, a team size, an end date for V1.3, or GPCP's share of the CISESS agreement.

Overall confidence: **moderate**. The people facts come from the institutions' own pages and ORCID/OpenAlex records. The ages are estimates, and the funding links are partly inferred.

## Team and succession

No source states a GPCP team size or a succession or knowledge-transfer plan (P-1: nothing published). The NOAA CDR Program's written R2O rules are the only knowledge-transfer requirement found:
- **IOC:** to reach Initial Operational Capability, the CDR must score "level 3 or greater" in every Maturity Matrix column, and the PI delivers source code to coding standard CDRP-STD-0007, a C-ATBD, a data-flow diagram and a maturity matrix, and passes an Operational Readiness Review ([NOAA, CDRP-PLAN-0017, 2014](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/Guidelines/Transitioning_CDRs_from_R2O.pdf)).
- **FOC:** at Full Operational Capability, "NCDC will be responsible for routinely and systematically generating CDR data" ([NOAA, CDRP-PRCS-0001, 2011](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/Guidelines/IOC_to_FOC_Transition.pdf)).

What NCEI holds is the 2017 V1.3 package (01B-35): the C-ATBD, data-flow diagram and maturity matrix (2017-08-14) and `SourceCode_01B-35.tar.gz` (2017-09-18). It holds no Operational Algorithm Description and nothing for V3.x ([NCEI CDR directory](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/CDRs/Precipitation_GPCP-Daily/)). Confidence: high.

For the problems:
- **P-3 (critical knowledge undocumented): partly contradicted for V1.3**, whose code and documents passed IOC delivery. NCEI's V1.3 record describes the code as "around 150 individual code files" in 6 modules plus 4 utility codes, run by one shell-script driver ([NCEI C00999](https://data.noaa.gov/waf/NOAA/NESDIS/ncei/aim/iso/xml/C00999.xml), process step ps001).
- The V3.3 record repeats that text word for word and links the same 2017 tarball, so it describes V1.3, not V3.3 ([NCEI C01726](https://data.noaa.gov/waf/NOAA/NESDIS/ncei/aim/iso/xml/C01726.xml)).
- **P-3: supported for V3.3**, which NCEI distributes as a CDR with no code at NCEI.
- **DIRS-4 (needs outside processing infrastructure):** the absence of an Operational Algorithm Description suggests GPCP Daily never reached FOC, so NCEI does not run it. That is an inference.

## Production

**Who computes the V3.3 copy at NCEI (conflict, unresolved).**
- NCEI's V3.3 metadata (dateStamp 2026-05-22) says: "The GPCP Daily V3.3 analysis is currently computed by the University of Maryland and submitted to NCEI." ([NCEI C01726](https://data.noaa.gov/waf/NOAA/NESDIS/ncei/aim/iso/xml/C01726.xml))
- Huffman's bio, written when V3.2 was current, says V3 products "are computed at Goddard and archived in" GES DISC ([GSFC](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/sci/bio/george.j.huffman)).
- The NCEI sentence is word for word the one in the V1.3 record ([NCEI C00999](https://data.noaa.gov/waf/NOAA/NESDIS/ncei/aim/iso/xml/C00999.xml)), so it may be inherited text.
- The likeliest reading is that Goddard runs the V3.x code and UMD/ESSIC (Adler, the NOAA CDR PI) is NCEI's producer of record. No source says so. Confidence: low.

**1DD V1.3.**
- NCEI's metadata says UMD computes it, two months after each month ends, status "onGoing".
- NCEI's [2026 folder](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/global-precipitation-climatology-project-gpcp-daily/access/2026/) ends with `gpcp_v01r03_daily_d20260831_c20261109.nc`, so production continues (high).
- No end date for V1.3 and no machine were found.
- Who runs V1.3 is also in conflict (unresolved): NCEI says UMD, while Huffman's job line says he produces the One-Degree Daily, and his bio lists V1.3 with himself as lead developer, "last updated 2024".
- The EGU26 abstract calls V3.3 Daily (1998–2024), "issued in February 2025", "the final generation before the transition to GPCP Version 4" ([EGU26-5885](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-5885.html)). It says nothing of V1.3's end.

**V3.3 restart.**
- The [Earthdata page](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/ges-disc-gpcpday-3.3) still says products are "currently paused at September 2024", yet it gives the extent as 1998-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
- NASA CMR shows the first post-pause granules (2024-10-01) [updated 2026-02-05](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPCPDAY&version=3.3&temporal=2024-10-01T00:00:00Z,2024-10-02T00:00:00Z&page_size=2). The [latest granule](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=GPCPDAY&version=3.3&sort_key=-start_date&page_size=2) (2025-12-31) was updated 2026-05-01.
- So the extension happened between February and May 2026. These are metadata update times, not a production log, and no notice was found (moderate).

**Computers.**
- No source says whether V3.x runs on Code 612 workstations or at NCCS. NCCS appears only as the host of V1.3 technical notes on its ESGF node.
- DIRS-4: nothing published.

**Code sharing.**
