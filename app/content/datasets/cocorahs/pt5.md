**Two inputs show an emerging threat, and none an imminent one, so the overall rating is emerging.** The first is the gauge supply. Productive Alternatives, the Fergus Falls maker of the Stratus gauge (the network's most common gauge), closed its production area between December 2024 and January 2026. The Stratus is still sold and, by a September 2026 account, still made in Minnesota by Darter Plastics. The second is the NWS offices that recruit observers: they lost staff in 2025, but no source shows any effect on CoCoRaHS. Observer counts were flat: 27,118 active stations in May 2024 and 27,623 in May 2026. The apps were replaced in-house, with no outages found. No substitute matches CoCoRaHS's density: COOP is itself under a proposed "right-sizing", and the densest gridded products either use CoCoRaHS or are coarser without it. **Overall confidence: moderate.**

## Observers: counts flat, recruiting softer (high confidence)

CoCoRaHS's own decks count stations that reported in the past 12 months, on May 1 each year:

- 2023: 25,987
- 2024: 27,118
- 2025: 27,676
- 2026: 27,623

The last year's change was −53, or −0.2% ([WERA 2024 deck, Spies](https://media.cocorahs.org/docs/WERA_2024_15_RecruitingRetention_Spies.pdf); [WCC 2026 deck, Spies](https://media.cocorahs.org/docs/WCC_2026_Day2_Spies_Recruiting_Retention.pdf)). The 2026 total splits into 26,758 in the USA, 833 in Canada and 32 in the Bahamas.

Recruiting weakened after 2024, without a fall in the total:

- **Spring contest.** New observers were 1,884 in 2024, 1,196 in 2025 and 1,544 in 2026, when the contest moved to April ([2024 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20240408164131/https://www.cocorahs.org/marchmadness.aspx), [2025 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20250416142125/https://www.cocorahs.org/marchmadness.aspx), [2026 results](https://www.cocorahs.org/marchmadness.aspx)).
- **New stations.** Stations created fell from 5,722 (year to May 2024) to 4,787 (year to May 2026).
- **First reports.** The share of new stations that sent a first report fell from 60% (year to May 2024) to 54% (year to May 2026). The 2024 deck itself warns that a recent sign-up "may have not yet started". No figure is given for the year to May 2025.
- **Reporting.** In calendar 2025, reporting observers grew 1.2% and daily reports fell 0.5%.
- **Figures that differ slightly.**
  - Each contest page gives two figures: new observers (1,196 in 2025, 1,544 in 2026) and a "Total Station Count" (1,194 and 1,542). The answer uses the new-observer figures.
  - The 2026 deck splits the 4,787 new stations by country two ways: USA 4,553, Canada 232 and Bahamas 2 on one slide, and USA 4,578, Canada 139 and Bahamas 3 (4,720 in all) on another. The answer uses only the total.

At the state level, Indiana's NWS page says most counties have fewer than the 10 observers wanted per county (as of 17 March 2026; [NWS Indianapolis](https://www.weather.gov/ind/incocorahs)). No source reports a decline in observers.

**Does it agree with GHCN-Daily?** Yes, in size, as far as it can be compared (moderate confidence). CoCoRaHS reports about 3,000 observers stopping each year ([WERA minutes 2020–22](https://nimss.org/storage/3841/WERA1012_Summary_of_Meeting_Minutes_2020-2022.pdf)). By last report year, 3,170 stations last reported in 2022 and 3,245 in 2024. CoCoRaHS publishes no figure for stations whose last report was in 2025. Its counts cover every station. GHCN-Daily keeps only stations with 100 or more values, so its turnover should be lower: about 1,785–2,078 a year, and 2,139 for 2025, from run `2026-11-21_ghcn-daily-sources-pt5`. Neither series shows a break in 2025.

## Gauge: a change of maker, not an end of supply (moderate confidence)

- **The closure.** Productive Alternatives' home page now says it "has officially closed its production area", and that Stratus gauges "are still available for purchase through Darter Plastics" ([productivemn.org](https://productivemn.org/), live 26 Nov 2026).
  - The notice was not there on 22 Dec 2024, when the page still called the Stratus "our most popular product" ([snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20241222022701/https://productivemn.org/)).
  - It was there on 25 Jan 2026 ([snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260125085932/https://productivemn.org/)).
  - No news report of the closure was found.
- **Darter Plastics.** It sells the Stratus as "our Stratus Rain Gauge" for $41.99 ([Darter/DPI Products](https://darterproducts.com/products/rain-gauge)). A 28 Sep 2026 post by its brand partner, FLI Products, says it is "made in the USA by Darter Plastics" and "manufactured in Minnesota" at the injection-mold plant that makes its other products ([FLI Products, 2026](https://www.fliproducts.com/blogs/america250-made-in-usa/america250-stratus-rain-gauge-made-in-usa-darter-plastics)). That is a reseller's marketing claim, so continued manufacture is moderate, not high, confidence.
- **CoCoRaHS's own page is out of date.** Its approved-gauge page still lists "Stratus – by Productive Alternatives" ([CoCoRaHS approved gauges](https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=approved_rain_gauges)).
- **A second maker exists.** CoCoRaHS also approves the TROPO by Climalytic Instruments, a premium gauge at about $89.99 ([Day Weather listing](https://www.dayweather.com/rain-gauge)), and the NWS 8-inch standard gauge, the costliest.
- **Retailers changed without any stock notice.** The store page's retailer list changed between 2024 and 2026 ([Jan 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240108103045/https://cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=store), [Jul 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250726103858/https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=store), [Nov 2026](https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=store)).
- **No shortage found.** No shortage, tariff or national price spike was found. A Lee Valley (Canada) listing that snippets called "Discontinued" now redirects to a category page, so it can't be confirmed.

## Website, app and hosting: replaced, not disrupted (high confidence)

- **Why the apps were replaced.** CoCoRaHS's 2024 IT update says the old apps "were created by an observer years ago. He no longer wants to maintain them" ([WERA 2024 IT update, Turner](https://media.cocorahs.org/docs/WERA_2024_10_IT_Update_Turner.pdf)).
  - CoCoRaHS released its own app in November 2024, a web app at mobile.cocorahs.org inside a store wrapper ([CoCoRaHS blog, 2024](https://cocorahs.blogspot.com/2024)).
  - The old Android app left Google Play on 5 Feb 2025 ([Chrome-Stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.appcay.cocorahs), an aggregator).
  - The new app was last updated on 31 Aug 2026 and has 5K+ downloads ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.cocorahs.mobile)).
- **Backup route.** The guide offers three routes with "the same user experience", the website among them ([user guide](https://media.cocorahs.org/docs/CoCoRaHS_MobileApp_User_Guide.pdf)). No phone, email or NWS route was found.
- **Hosting.** Systems were migrated to Azure ([NIMSS 2025 report](https://nimss.org/seas/52786)).
- **Maps.** In November 2026, CoCoRaHS began moving its map to MapHub, partly from "a need to reduce our costs" ([CoCoRaHS blog, 2026](https://cocorahs.blogspot.com/2026/11/the-latest-cocorahs-innovation-maphub.html)).
- **No outage found.** No 2024–2026 outage was found. The single-developer risk found in Part 3 is unchanged.

## Recruiters: NWS losses, no shown effect (moderate confidence)

- **NWS staffing.**
  - In April 2025, nearly half of the 122 forecast offices had vacancy rates of 20% or more ([AP via PBS, 2025](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/nearly-half-of-national-weather-service-offices-are-critically-understaffed-experts-warn)).
  - NWS lost 278 staff in the first cuts ([E&E News, Mar 2025](https://www.eenews.net/articles/nws-offices-face-vacancy-crisis-as-more-cuts-loom-union-says/)), and more than 600 in all in 2025. By June 2026 it had rehired about 274 ([E&E News, Jun 2026](https://www.eenews.net/articles/national-weather-service-in-transition-as-hurricane-season-begins/)).
  - Commerce OIG opened a staffing audit in July 2025 ([OIG memo](https://www.oig.doc.gov/wp-content/OIGPublications/2025-07-17-NWS-Staffing-Announcement-SECURED.pdf)). No final report was found.
- **NWS offices kept recruiting.** No source ties these losses to CoCoRaHS, and offices ran CoCoRaHS drives in 2025 and 2026 ([NWS Mobile, Apr 2026](https://www.weather.gov/mob/CoCoRaHSRecruitingBlitz2026); [Detroit Free Press, Mar 2025](https://www.yahoo.com/news/national-weather-needs-volunteers-including-171535171.html)).
- **Volunteer coordinators.**
  - New York had county coordinators in 26 of its 62 counties ([NY newsletter, Jan 2025](https://media.cocorahs.org/docs/NY_CoCo_Newsletter_Jan2025.pdf)).
  - Texas lists vacant county posts ([Texas coordinators](https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=coord_tx)).
  - The 2025 WCC-1012 meeting was replaced by an internal one, because many participants could not attend "due to constraints and uncertainty" ([NIMSS 2025 report](https://nimss.org/seas/52786); [committee page](https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=wera1012)).
- **Doesken.** Founder Nolan Doesken died on 15 Nov 2026 ([MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/11/21/precipitation-collection-network-pioneer-nolan-doesken-remembered-by-weather-community)). He had already retired.

## Replacements if an input failed

- **Gauge:** a second gauge supplier exists. Stratus supply moved to Darter Plastics, and the TROPO (Climalytic) and the NWS 8-inch gauge are approved alternatives, at higher cost.
- **Data entry:** the website is a full backup for the app. The new app itself is the website, and the old apps have no backup. No offline route was found.
- **Recruiters:** CoCoRaHS state coordinators, often at state climate offices, and its own national contests recruit independently of NWS. No replacement exists for a lost county coordinator except new volunteers.

## The DIRS risks

| Risk | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| DIRS-1 external calibration service | **Not supported** | Gauges are built to the NWS standard, and no external calibration service enters the supply. The maker change didn't involve one. |
| DIRS-2 upstream networks or platforms aging out or losing funding | **Mixed** | Contradicted for observers (flat counts) and the apps (replaced). Supported for the recruiters (NWS staff losses) and for the substitutes (COOP right-sizing, mesonet grant cuts). |
| DIRS-3 upstream data feeds vulnerable | **Contradicted (weakly)** | No outage was found, and the web route backs the app. Single-developer and single-host dependence remain, as found in Part 3. |
| DIRS-5 no alternative supplier | **Mostly contradicted** | Three approved gauges from different makers, and Stratus supply continued after its maker left production. It still holds partly: the cheap, most common gauge has one maker. |

## Gaps and uncertainties

- **Closure date and tooling:** the exact month Productive Alternatives stopped production, and whether Darter took over the molds, are not published. Productive Alternatives or Darter Plastics (763-261-5000) hold this, or CoCoRaHS HQ (info@cocorahs.org), whose gauge page has not been updated.
- **2025 turnover:** CoCoRaHS's count of stations whose last report was in 2025 is not published. It is kept in CoCoRaHS's station database and would appear in a WCC 2027 deck, likely around May 2027.
- **COOP counts by year:** no series was found. The counts conflict: about 6,700 sites (Federal Register 2026), roughly 9,000 published sites (HOMR, undated), and 7,786 lines in HOMR's current list (counting territories and sites that report hourly precipitation only). NWS holds them, and its response to the May 2026 request for comments is not yet out.
- **Pages not read:** the Stage IV gauge inputs were not read, and whether current MRMS takes CoCoRaHS through MADIS was not settled. The OIG audit 2025-523 has no final report yet.
