**Direct answer.** No public source says what pays for producing PERSIANN-CDR Daily today. The record was built in 2009–2014 with NOAA Climate Data Record (CDR) Program money, passed through a cooperative-institute sub-award to UC Irvine's Center for Hydrometeorology and Remote Sensing (CHRS). Since then, CHRS has kept extending it, with no budget line, contract or award that names the work. The group's recent money comes from NOAA, NASA, CISESS/JPSS and California grants for related CHRS products.

From January 2025 to November 2026, no cut, termination or proposal aimed at PERSIANN-CDR or at the CHRS precipitation grants was found. Overall rating: **no current threat found to the dataset's own funding, with two emerging threats around it**:
- the FY2027 request cuts NCEI, the host, by $17.8M (to $52.0M; the same level the FY2026 request proposed, which Congress didn't enact);
- CHRS's main NOAA grant ended on 2026-08-31, and no successor has been found.

Confidence: **moderate**. The finding rests partly on absence: the route that pays for production is not public.

## How production is paid for

- **Original development (2009–2014): NOAA CDR Program, through CICS.** The founding paper credits "the NOAA NCDC/Climate Data Record program (Prime Award NA09NES440006 and NCSU CICS Sub-Award 2009-1380-01)" ([Ashouri et al., BAMS 2015](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-13-00068.1)).
  - That prime award is the University of Maryland's whole CICS agreement: $66,720,558 obligated, 2009-07-01 to 2014-06-30 ([USAspending NA09NES4400006](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_NA09NES4400006_1330/)).
  - The award number differs between the papers. The 2015 paper prints it with one zero fewer than USAspending. The 2019 paper (Sadeghi et al.) prints NA09NES4400006, matching USAspending, so the 2015 form is taken as a misprint.
  - The UCI sub-award's amount is not published. **High** confidence.
- **The program's design, and the program's end.**
  - NOAA's 2011 transition rules say the CDR Program "competitively selects Principal Investigators" to develop a record. Once operational, routine production "may take place within NCDC or at another suitable facility" ([NCDC CDRP IOC-to-FOC, 2011](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/Guidelines/IOC_to_FOC_Transition.pdf)).
  - A 2019 AMS abstract by NCEI staff says "During the NCEI reorganization in 2015, the CDR Program ended". Upkeep of the records moved into NCEI's centers ([AMS 2019](https://ams.confex.com/ams/2019Annual/webprogram/Paper352356.html)).
  - NCEI's December 2025 user guide still calls PERSIANN-CDR part of "NOAA's Climate Data Record (CDR) Program", with quarterly updates "produced and delivered" ([NCEI CDR documents](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/sds/cdr/CDRs/PERSIANN/)). It doesn't say who produces them or who pays.
  - **Moderate** confidence.
- **CHRS still produces it.**
  - The newest files, created 2026-10-13 and running to 2026-07-31, name a UC Irvine creator and "UC-IRVINE/CHRS" as the institution ([NCEI ERDDAP](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/erddap/info/cdr_persiann_by_time_lon_lat/index.html); [NCEI 2026 folder](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/precipitation-persiann/access/2026/)).
  - CHRS's own data folders were updated the same day ([CHRS server](https://persiann.eng.uci.edu/CHRSdata/PERSIANN-CDR/)).
  - The files' project attribute is "Satellite Data Support for Hydrologic and Water Resources Planning and Management. Sponsor: NOAA". No award record with that title was found. **High** confidence that CHRS produces it; the funding behind that work is unknown.
- **No budget line.** NOAA's FY2026 and FY2027 congressional justifications have no line for "Climate Data Records" or PERSIANN. Neither do the Senate FY2026 report or the FY2026 explanatory statement ([NOAA budget documents](https://www.noaa.gov/organization/budget-finance-performance/budget-and-reports); [S. Rept. 119-44](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-report/119th-congress/senate-report/44)). Any money for the record sits inside larger NESDIS/NCEI lines.
