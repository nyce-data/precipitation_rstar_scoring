**Overall rating: emerging.** Nothing enacted or ordered has cut USCRN, ARL or the daily01 product since January 2025. The threat is a closure of ARL, and of "a nation-wide network of soil moisture sensors", proposed in two budget requests in a row. Congress rejected it for FY2026, and both FY2027 committee bills reject it again, but FY2027 rests on a continuing resolution that ends December 11, 2026. Meanwhile ATDD has been folded into another ARL division, and the field team now works under a cooperative-institute arrangement. daily01 itself kept updating every day, through the October–November 2025 shutdown and NCEI's cloud migration. **Confidence: moderate to high** (high for funding status and access; moderate for staff, where few figures are published).

## Funding

- **FY2027 is not settled.** No FY2027 CJS law has passed. NOAA runs on Division A of P.L. 119-103 at the FY2026 rate until December 11, 2026. Its only NOAA anomaly is for the GeoXO satellites; none covers OAR, ARL or the soil-moisture sensors ([CRS R49353, 2026](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-09-17_R49353_98ddd8e7b1852b47b24e394ed7ddf6d6d53b4632.html)). The Senate committee only posted its CJS text on 2026-11-12, with no markup found ([Senate Appropriations, 2026](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/news/majority/senate-appropriations-committee-releases-five-fy-2027-funding-bills)). **High.**
- **Both FY2027 committee bills keep the labs.** The House report gives Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes $92,500K, against $103,877K enacted for FY2026; the minority calls that an 11% cut. It again "directs NOAA to maintain these capabilities and to avoid closures, consolidations, or eliminations" ([H.Rept. 119-652](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119hrpt652/html/CRPT-119hrpt652.htm)). The Senate draft gives $104,602K and "rejects the proposed terminations" ([Senate explanatory statement, 2026](https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/cjs_explanatory_stmt.pdf)). Neither names ARL, ATDD or USCRN. **High.**
- **The FY2027 request repeats the FY2026 closure plan.** It terminates Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes (−$104,964K, −180 FTE, −211 positions) and says NOAA will close ARL in College Park, Idaho Falls and Oak Ridge "as well as a nation-wide network of soil moisture sensors". It gives no separate figure for ARL or the sensors and doesn't name USCRN ([NOAA FY2027 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)). **High.**
- **FY2026 spend plan: not found.** P.L. 119-74's explanatory statement gives Climate Laboratories and Cooperative Institutes $104,102K and tells NOAA to follow its table and narrative ([Congressional Record H255, 2026-01-08](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-01-08/html/CREC-2026-01-08-pt1-PgH255.htm)). Sec. 528 requires spending plans, but none is public, and no letter or report says the FY2026 plan cuts ARL. For FY2025, by contrast, senators said NOAA's plan was $246M below what Congress directed, with labs and cooperative institutes affected ([AIP, 2025](https://www.aip.org/fyi/democrats-accuse-administration-of-cutting-spending-at-nasa-and-noaa)). **Moderate.**
- **A risk to watch, not tied to USCRN by any source:** OMB paused NOAA grants, cooperative-institute grants included, from February 27 until April 2026 ([Boulder Reporting Lab, 2026](https://boulderreportinglab.org/2026/04/02/half-of-staff-at-boulders-noaa-global-monitoring-lab-face-furloughs-as-funding-freeze-drags-on/)). Because the USCRN field team now sits under a cooperative institute (Staff, below), a repeat pause could reach it.
- **Network downsizing:** nothing newer than the 2025 NOAA report was found. The only earlier move was NOAA's November 2023 notice that it would retire 113 soil-moisture sensors, which it delayed after Sen. Cruz's inquiry ([Senate Commerce, 2024](https://www.commerce.senate.gov/press/rep/release/on-behalf-of-texas-ag-sen-cruz-secures-extension-of-drought-warning-sensors-2024-2/)). **Moderate.**

## Staff

- **ATDD is no longer a division.** On 2025-10-01 ARL's directory listed ATDD as its own division with Kochendorfer as Division Director. The live directory now lists a "SORD-Atmospheric Turbulence and Diffusion (SORD-ATD) office" in Oak Ridge inside the Special Research and Operations Division (director Walter Schalk), with Kochendorfer as Division Deputy ([ARL staff directory, 2026](https://www.arl.noaa.gov/about/staff-directory-2/); [Wayback, 2025-10-01](https://web.archive.org/web/20251001031801/https://www.arl.noaa.gov/about/staff-directory/)). No announcement or date was found. **Moderate.**
- **The field team's label changed, but probably not its route.** ARL's directory listed Brent French and the field staff as ORAU in December 2024 and as "Cooperative Institute" by October 2025 ([Wayback, 2024-12-13](https://web.archive.org/web/20241213/https://www.arl.noaa.gov/about/staff-directory/); [Wayback, 2025-10-01](https://web.archive.org/web/20251001031801/https://www.arl.noaa.gov/about/staff-directory/)).
  - CISESS's 2022–23 annual report already lists ORAU's ATDD/USCRN support as CISESS task NC-SAS-06-ORAU ([CISESS NC, 2023](https://ncics.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CISESS%20NC%20Annual%20Report%202022%E2%80%932023.pdf)).
  - ORAU's 2025 report says it "will continue to maintain and expand" USCRN "through a contract extension" with ATDD and the Cooperative Institute for Satellite Earth System Studies (CISESS) at NC State ([ORAU Story 2025](https://orau.org/news/resources/2025-orau-story.pdf)).
  - CISESS's old umbrella agreement (NA19NES4320002) ended 2025-06-30 ([USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/search/spending_by_award/)). UMD announced a five-year, $388M renewal on 2024-07-15, without an award number ([UMD, 2024](https://today.umd.edu/umd-awarded-388m-noaa-cooperative-agreement-for-earth-system-studies)).
  - So the "Cooperative Institute" is CISESS, and ORAU is still involved; the 2025 label likely tracks the CISESS renewal rather than a new employer. **Moderate:** the post-June-2025 contract chain and headcount aren't published.
- **The field team still works.** ARL says "a crew of five technicians and engineers" replaced GOES transmitters at 139 towers in 45 states, the last on 2026-04-25. 94 old transmitters were still in the field in January 2025, and the deadline was May 31, 2026 ([ARL, 2026-07-23](https://www.arl.noaa.gov/news-pubs/arl-news-stories/upgrading-arls-largest-meteorological-network/)). This crew of five is a subset of the field team; Part 3 counted 8 in the directory. **High.**
- **No staff-loss numbers for ARL, ATDD or USCRN are published.** Context only, not tied to USCRN by any source:
  - OPM data show about 20% job loss at NOAA since January 2025, with no RIF separations recorded and a hiring freeze from January 2025 ([AIP, 2026](https://www.aip.org/fyi/federal-science-workforce-declines-sharply-under-trump));
  - NCEI lost 58 employees, about a third, by spring 2025, according to a retiring NCEI official ([Asheville Watchdog, 2025](https://avlwatchdog.org/top-climate-scientists-departure-is-sign-of-brain-drain-at-noaa-hub-in-asheville-and-of-deep-cuts-to-research-across-the-country/));
  - Commerce's workforce fell from 48,804 (Dec 2024) to 41,606 (Jan 2026) ([GAO-26-108583](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108583.pdf));
  - OAR staff were told in April 2025 that web-hosting contracts were likely to be cancelled under the Commerce contract review ([NPR, 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/04/09/nx-s1-5356166/noaa-contracts-reviewed-one-by-one)).

  Part 3's directory count (28 ATDD entries in Dec 2024, 21 in Nov 2026) remains the only ATDD-specific figure. **Moderate.**
- Diamond is still named USCRN Program Manager by both ARL and NCEI.
