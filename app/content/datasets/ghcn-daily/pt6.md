**Direct answer.** GHCN-Daily now depends on one named federal scientist, Matthew J. Menne. Co-principal investigator Imke Durre left NCEI on 30 April 2025, according to her own ORCID record. The other hands-on staff who have been named are mostly Riverside Technology contractors: Bryant Korzeniewski, Nancy Casey and probably Shelley McNeill. On my estimates, which come from publication years and not from personnel records, the branch chief above the team (Russell Vose) is probably already eligible to retire, and Menne may become eligible within 2 years. No succession, workforce or knowledge-transfer plan has been published, and the processing and QC code is neither public nor, as of 2021, under configuration management. The routine daily updates would keep running without staff. Adding new sources and maintaining the QC depend on very few people. **Confidence: moderate** on who is there and the gaps in documentation; **low** on the retirement estimates.

## Team, succession and oversight

- **P-1 (no documented succession plan): supported, since nothing is published.** NOAA's workforce-planning order (NAO 202-1103, issued 2007, "Reviewed Last: In Process") lists succession planning only among "possible strategies" ([NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov/organization/administration/nao-202-1103-noaa-workforce-planning-policy)). No NCEI, NESDIS or GHCN-Daily plan turned up. Generic guidance does exist: search results show a NOAA "Workforce Succession Planning Toolkit" (2012) and a Commerce "OHCS Succession Planning Guide" (2022), both unread. These **partly contradict** a literal reading of P-1 at the agency level, but neither is a plan for GHCN-Daily. Commerce's Jan 2026 staffing-plan framework, which uses retirement-eligibility data, was seen only as a snippet (PDF 403). *Moderate.*
- **Oversight bodies say nothing about NCEI.** Commerce OIG's FY2026 challenges report covers NWS ("approximately 13 percent of its 4,369 employees" left Jan–Apr 2025), ship crews and aircraft, not NCEI ([OIG-26-003](https://www.oig.doc.gov/wp-content/OIGPublications/OIG-26-003_TMC_FY_2026_SECURED-1.pdf)). GAO's NOAA workforce reports cover hurricane hunters, NWS aviation meteorologists and OMAO mariners.
- **2025 departures.** On 28 Feb 2025, 20 NCEI employees ("about 10 percent of the Center's authorized staff") left, including two "retirement eligible" senior scientists and nine deferred resignations. No GHCN-Daily staff are named. The same article reports rumors of cuts "as deep as 40 percent of the authorized level" ([Asheville Watchdog, 2025](https://avlwatchdog.org/it-is-a-dark-time-trump-cuts-deeply-into-staff-of-asheville-scientists-who-maintain-worlds-largest-archive-of-environmental-records/)). Durre's 30 April 2025 exit is the only departure among the named people found since 2024. Stevens left in 2021. *Moderate* (single news source for the count).

Integrating sources, handling Datzilla tickets and changing the QC do need people, and those rest on Menne plus contractors. The published methods papers and readme are the main written record.
