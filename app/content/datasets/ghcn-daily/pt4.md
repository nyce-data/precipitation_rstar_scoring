**Direct answer.** No source found proposes to end or cut GHCN-Daily itself, and no news item names its team or any team member. The funding risk is at the level of NCEI's budget line. The FY2027 request would cut NCEI by about 25% ($69.8M to $52.0M). Congress's committee marks mostly reject that cut ($60.0M House, $70.0M Senate), but nothing is enacted: FY2027 runs on a stopgap to December 11, 2026, and another stopgap was expected. Funding overall is rated **emerging**. Two developments name GHCN-Daily directly and are **imminent** (already happening): degraded inputs since late August 2025, and access and update delays from NCEI's cloud move in November 2026. Overall confidence is moderate.

This adds to the earlier runs ([Part 3 funding route](../2026-11-21_ghcn-daily-funding-pt3/answer.md), [combined funding and threats](../2026-11-19_ghcn-daily-about-pt3_combined/answer.md), [NCEI threats](../2026-11-17_noaa-ncei-threats/answer.md)). ## Funding

- **FY2027 request (April 2026).** NOAA's justification cuts the NCEI subactivity from $69,807K to $52,000K (−$17,807K), keeping 185 positions and 140 FTE. The narrative says NOAA would "reduce engagement services, tailored products, and eliminate the Regional Climate Services program", and it names no GHCN product ([NOAA FY2027 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/FY2027-NOAA-CJ-Submission.pdf)). The same table gives an FY2025 actual of $76,035K with 126 positions on board, against 185 enacted positions. The CJ doesn't say what the FY2025 actual includes (carryover, for example). **High** (the agency's own table).
- **Congress (May–November 2026).** House H.Rept. 119-652 recommends $60,000K for NCEI ([House report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-119hrpt652/html/CRPT-119hrpt652.htm)). ## Consequences for updates, completeness and availability

- **Completeness (imminent, partly recovering).** Berkeley Earth's figures are its own intake from all sources, not NOAA counts. UCS's "6,000 of 20,000" repeats Berkeley's early February 2026 count, which Berkeley later revised to ~10,000; it gave ~6,700 for March. NOAA has given no cause. NCEI's own [status log](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/status.txt) shows repairs:
  - v3.33 (January 5, 2026) added SSODv2, "the successor dataset to GSOD";
  - v3.34 (January 23, 2026) added a further NWS source;
  - v3.35 (October 1, 2026) "resumes updates for Environment Canada stations", whose daily feed had been "unexpectedly discontinued on April 28, 2024", and starts weekly re-integration of their full record;
  - earlier, v3.32 (March 25, 2025) added short-delay U.S. airport summaries from the High Plains Regional Climate Center, and the European ECA&D source, static since September 20, 2024, resumed monthly updates on January 20, 2025.

  The last full reprocessing was 3.35-por-2026110623 (November 6, 2026).

  The log posts no outage for the 2025 shutdown. **Moderate.**
- **A likely cause (interpretation, not stated by any source).** The degradation began in late August 2025, the same date that NCEI's ISD and GSOD "reached end of life on August 29, 2025" ([pygsod](https://github.com/jmarrec/pygsod); [GSODR on CRAN](https://cran.r-project.org/web//packages/GSODR/GSODR.pdf); both snippet only). Both were GHCN-Daily sources, and NCEI's January 2026 fix added GSOD's successor. That points to the product transition rather than staffing or funding, but no NOAA or press source says so. **Low.**
- **Availability and updates (imminent, short term).** The GHCN-Daily product page and an [NCEI alert](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/alerts) (November 16–30, 2026) warn of "delayed data archival and slower update cycles". On November 21, 2026, this run loaded the data folder. Its [version file](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-version.txt) showed an update that started at 2026111517 UTC, about six days old for a product normally updated daily. The 404s reported on November 18 suggest access comes and goes. **High.**
- **If the FY2027 request were enacted,** NCEI would lose a quarter of its line while keeping its 185 positions on paper. The request names engagement, tailored products and Regional Climate Services, not GHCN. What would happen to GHCN-Daily's staff time is not stated anywhere. **Low** (an inference from the CJ's wording).
