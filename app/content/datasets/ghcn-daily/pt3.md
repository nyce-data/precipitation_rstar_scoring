GHCN-Daily is made by NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) and paid for mainly out of one federal line: the NCEI subactivity in NESDIS's Operations, Research, and Facilities (ORF) appropriation. That line is set each year and its money stays available for two years. Smaller federal routes add to it: NOAA Climate Program Office (CPO) funding, CISESS cooperative-institute tasks and support contracts. Much of the input data comes in kind from other agencies and national weather services. NOAA publishes no figure for GHCN-Daily's own share of any route. The NCEI line's enacted amount rose from $61.6M (FY2020) to $71.4M (FY2023), then held at $69–70M through FY2026, so the structure rates **narrow but steady** for FY2020–FY2026. Confidence: high for the line's figures, moderate for the smaller routes and for where the team sits.

## Who does the work

- **NCEI (NOAA/NESDIS) does the production.** The dataset's metadata names the "GHCNd Operations Team" as point of contact, and NOAA's 2021 stewardship review calls the dataset "operational and supported by NCEI" ([NCEI ISO metadata C00861](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/metadata/geoportal/rest/metadata/item/gov.noaa.ncdc:C00861/html); [NESDIS DSMR-00227, 2021](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/34330/noaa_34330_DS1.pdf)). *High.*
- **Unit: the Climatic Science & Services Division, placed by inference.** No page names the unit for GHCNd. NCEI's February 2023 chart puts a Dataset Section and a Products Section under the Climatic Science & Development Branch of that division ([NCEI org chart, 2023](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/s3dl?path=%2Fs3%2F2023-02%2FNCEI_Org_Chart_Feb_2023.pdf)). A 2023 NWS seminar bio places Matt Menne, GHCN's "technical and scientific lead", in the Dataset Section ([NWS Climate Services Seminars, 2023](https://www.weather.gov/climateservices/seminars)). The metadata names Menne and Imke Durre (the Products Section chief in that chart) as principal investigators. No chart newer than 2023 was found, and team size is unpublished. *Moderate.*
- **CISESS (NC State's NCICS in Asheville; the agreement is held by the University of Maryland)** does development tasks, not routine production. Examples are a cloud processing prototype for GHCN-D (task NC-SON-02-NCICS-BB, 2019–20) and a GHCN-D graph database (2021–23) ([CICS-NC final report, 2020](https://ncics.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/CICS-NC-2020-Final-Report.pdf); [CISESS NC annual report 2022–23](https://ncics.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CISESS%20NC%20Annual%20Report%202022%E2%80%932023.pdf)). *Moderate.*
- **Contractors:** NCEI's science-support delivery order 1332KP24F0017 (Consolidated Safety Services) and data-stewardship call order 1332KP24F0001 (Alpha Omega Integration; 2024-02-04 to 2028-02-03; $19,119,736.87 obligated, $39,485,475.04 with all options) do not name GHCN, so their role, if any, is unknown ([USAspending 1332KP24F0017](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/CONT_AWD_1332KP24F0017_1330_1305M423DNEEA0014_1330/)).
- **In-kind data:** NCEI gets foreign station data from national meteorological and hydrological services "through official bi-lateral agreements" ([NCEI GHCNd product page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/global-historical-climatology-network-daily)). US inputs are paid for by their own programs: NWS COOP through the NWS ORF Observations line ([NOAA FY2026 CJ](https://www.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/NOAA%20FY26%20Congressional%20Justification.pdf)), and CoCoRaHS and USDA SNOTEL through their own funders.

For the sister product GHCN-Hourly, NCEI cites an in-kind agreement with Copernicus C3S and Atlas 15/BIL funding. Neither is a current GHCN-Daily route.

- **Staffing on the line fell while the dollars held.** Enacted positions went from 225 (FY2020) to 185 (FY2026), and FTE from 222 to 140. Actual positions were below the enacted figures in every year shown. Why is part of the separate cuts question.

## How stable the structure is

- **Narrow:** almost all of NCEI's production money comes through one annual ORF line, and GHCN-Daily has no line item, floor or earmark of its own. The explanatory statements set floors only for other NCEI work, such as Regional Climate Services. The multi-year pieces don't change this. The CISESS agreement runs to 2029 but is funded in yearly increments from the same NOAA appropriations and pays for development tasks. COM is competitive money and undated after 2019.
- **Steady:** enacted dollars rose 15.8% from FY2020 to FY2023, then sat 1.9% (FY2024) and 3.3% (FY2026) below FY2023 (computed from the table, nominal). The full-year CR in FY2025 kept the FY2024 level.
- **Rating: narrow but steady** for FY2020–FY2026 enacted funding. The falling position and FTE counts on the same line are the main sign against "steady"; they belong to the separate cuts-and-staffing question.

## Gaps

- **GHCN-Daily's share of the NCEI line, of COM and of CISESS:** not published. NCEI keeps it in its internal spend plan; ask ncdc.ghcnd@noaa.gov or file a FOIA request for NCEI's operating plan by product.
- **The COM transfer to NCEI's Applied Research Center:** no amount or year-by-year figure is public. It would sit in NOAA's internal spend plans (OAR and NCEI); COM's public list holds only outside grants.
- **Oversight:** no GAO or Commerce OIG report on NCEI or GHCN funding was found for 2018–2024 (2–3 queries on each site). Three related reports were found but not opened: OIG-24-034-A (NESDIS cloud), OIG-16-043-A (NESDIS cyber incident) and GAO-16-37 (climate information).
- **Not found or not checked:** FY2022 enacted positions; the FY2020 law number; an NCEI org chart after 2023. The CR counts are government-wide (CRS), not a CJS-specific table.
